Your four questions, answered in the order you asked them.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Fee structurePure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. Rates for claims against individual drivers are set after we review a representative sample of your accounts, and we walk through them on a call rather than in writing.
2. Skip tracing & contactEvery account is scored first, then traced for a current address, phone and email through multiple sources, and refreshed as drivers move. Compliant outreach runs across text, email, live and AI-assisted calls, voicemail and mailed notices, timed to when each account engages.

Eligibility depends on how each claim is classified and on completing validation, so we confirm it per account and never promise a credit outcome.

Per account we ask for the signed agreement, deduction authorization, itemized settlement statement, separation date, last-known address, phone and email, and records for any equipment, escrow, advance or fuel card balance. Outreach begins within 24–48 hours of a documented placement.
DisputesA dispute pauses recovery; it doesn’t end it. We validate against the paper — the agreement, the authorization and the statement — which is why the file you described matters.

Why placing sooner matters

Why fresh balances recover best

A balance is most collectible while it’s fresh. Once a driver has left, contact data goes stale quickly, and every month erodes leverage. The highest-leverage change most carriers make isn’t chasing harder — it’s placing sooner.

The age curve

Fresh accounts (under ~90 days past due) often resolve in 30–60 days; balances that sit a year collect a fraction of that.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than letting balances drift toward write-off — and on contingency there’s no cost to placing.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked.
