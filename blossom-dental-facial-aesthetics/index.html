03 — Your questions, answered

Your questions — answered directly

Everything you asked, grouped and answered in order. Exact rates are quoted after we’ve seen a representative sample of your aging — never off a rate card, because the right number depends on the age and mix of what you place.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Fees, minimums & contractPure contingency — $0 upfront, no setup or onboarding fee, no monthly fee, no monthly minimum, and no minimum number or dollar amount of accounts to submit. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. No long-term lock-in and no cancellation or termination fee — you can stop placing at any time. We quote your rate once we’ve reviewed a representative sample of your aging.
2. How the rate is setThe contingency rate reflects the age, balance and volume of what you place — fresher, larger, higher-volume books earn better rates. There is no fixed per-account fee. During scoping we’ll advise a practical minimum balance worth placing, so you’re not paying to chase dollars that cost more to work than they return.
3. Submitting accounts & Open DentalBulk-upload an aging export from Open Dental (CSV or spreadsheet) through our secure portal — we map your columns, so there’s no re-keying. Documented placements begin working within 24–48 hours, and onboarding is typically a few business days from signed agreement to first outreach.
4. Portal, remittance & direct payYou get a secure client portal to monitor every individual account and all recovery activity in real time. Recovered funds are remitted to the practice on a set monthly cycle with a full reconciliation statement. When a patient pays your office directly after placement, you log it in the portal so we stop contact immediately; because the account was placed, the agreed contingency applies to those payments too — standard, and it’s what keeps patients from being over-contacted.
5. Payment plans, skip tracing & credit reportingWe set up patient payment arrangements directly — often the fastest path to recovery on a patient balance. Skip tracing to locate patients who’ve moved is included in the contingency, never billed separately. Where you choose to enable it, we report to Equifax and Experian after the FCRA / Reg F validation window, on confirmed and undisputed balances only.
6. Uncollectable accounts & legalAn account that resists the standard process can move to continued monitoring, credit-reporting leverage, or attorney review — nothing happens without your approval. Legal / attorney forwarding is available for qualifying balances; any court costs or attorney fees are disclosed and approved in advance, never a surprise. On accounts we simply can’t collect, there is no fee — no recovery, no fee.
7. Recall, HIPAA & what to expectYou can recall or withdraw an account at any time; a fee applies only where we’ve already collected or a payment arrangement is in place. We operate under a signed BAA with encrypted transfer, access controls and staff training for dental / healthcare data. Recovery tracks account age closely — fresh balances recover materially higher than seasoned ones — so we give you an honest, age-based read on a representative sample, plus a sample agreement and full fee schedule, before you commit.

The age curve

Why the date you place matters more than the effort you spend

A patient balance is most collectible while it’s fresh. Every month it ages, contact data goes stale, the relationship cools, and recovery odds fall. The highest-leverage change a practice can make isn’t chasing harder — it’s placing the accounts your team can’t resolve sooner.

The decay curve

Fresh patient balances (inside ~90 days) recover several times higher than balances left to sit past a year — a steep, well-documented drop.

A practical option

Many practices keep the full internal cycle on most accounts but place a first-pass tier earlier on clear non-responders — and on contingency there’s no cost to placing.

Recovery varies by book; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked.
