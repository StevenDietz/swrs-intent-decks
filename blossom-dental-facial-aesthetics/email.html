Thank you for reaching out on behalf of Blossom Dental and Facial Aesthetics — and for such a thorough list. It tells me you’re doing this the right way. I’ve put together a short overview built around exactly what you asked, and I’ll answer the headline questions here so you have them in writing.

First, the honest part, because it should shape your expectations. You already run a disciplined in-house cycle — statements and 30-, 60- and 90-day notices — and you only want to place what’s left after that. That’s the right approach, and it also means the accounts you’d send us are already seasoned. Aged patient balances recover at a materially lower rate than fresh ones — not because the paper is worse, but because patients move and phone numbers change. So the work on a post-internal book is address and phone verification and skip tracing first, persistent multi-channel contact second. I’d rather give you an honest, age-based read on a representative sample than a headline number that flatters us.

On cost: pure contingency. $0 upfront, no setup or onboarding fee, no monthly fee, no monthly minimum, and no minimum number or dollar amount of accounts to submit. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover — so there’s no risk in placing and no cost if we don’t collect. There’s no long-term lock-in and no cancellation or termination fee. We quote your rate, and send a sample agreement and full fee schedule, once we’ve reviewed a representative sample of your aging — the right percentage depends on the age, balance and volume of what you place, which is why we don’t work off a rate card.

The rest of your list, briefly: you place by bulk-uploading an aging export from Open Dental through our secure client portal — we map your columns, so there’s no re-keying — and work begins within 24–48 hours of a documented placement. The portal gives you real-time, account-level visibility on every patient and all recovery activity. Skip tracing is included in the contingency, never billed separately. We set up patient payment arrangements directly. Where you choose to enable it, we report to Equifax and Experian after the required validation window on confirmed, undisputed balances. Recovered funds are remitted on a set monthly cycle with a full reconciliation statement; if a patient pays your office directly after placement, you log it in the portal so we stop contact immediately. You can recall an account at any time, and legal/attorney referral is available for qualifying balances with any court costs disclosed and approved in advance. We operate under a signed HIPAA Business Associate Agreement with encrypted transfer and access controls. The overview walks through every one of your questions in order.

We’ve collected millions of dollars in industries just like yours since 2004. If it’s ever useful, we also handle outsourced AR management and insurance follow-up — so balances can be worked well before they ever reach third-party placement.
