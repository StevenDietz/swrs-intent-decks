01 — Introduction

A recovery partner for the balances that leave with the resident

To Noah Webster and the Kingsley Management reconciliations team — across 73 communities, the hardest dollars to recover are the ones owed by a household that has already moved out: final lot rent, unpaid utilities and fees, and damages assessed after move-out, owed by someone your on-site team no longer has a reason to see.

Former-resident balances aren’t a leasing problem — they’re a reconciliation problem. Every move-out ledger that ages past the point your community managers can chase it quietly becomes a write-off, while the same household applies to live somewhere else.

Southwest Recovery Services is an outsourced receivables recovery partner working residential and property-management portfolios on pure contingency since 2004. You place by secure batch upload straight from your in-house platform — CSV or Excel, your columns, no re-keying. No recovery, no fee.

Your three criteria — answered directly

Exactly what you asked, in order, plus the detail that matters for a multi-state manufactured-housing portfolio.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Multi-state coverageWe recover residential and former-resident balances across the large majority of the United States, and we will be exact with you rather than easy: SWRS does not take consumer placements in California, Oregon or Washington, and Minnesota is restricted. Every other state in your footprint is serviceable today. Tell us the state distribution of the 73 communities and we will mark the file precisely before you place a single account.
2. 100% contingencyPure contingency — $0 upfront, no setup fee, no retainer, no monthly minimum, no per-account charge, and no court or legal costs billed to you. Recovery is out of court. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what is actually recovered, quoted once we have seen a representative sample of your move-out ledgers and their aging.
3. Batch placement from your platformStandard intake. Export from your in-house property management system as CSV or Excel and upload it through the client portal; we map your columns to ours, so nothing is re-keyed and no format change is required on your side. And if any of your communities run on ManageAmerica, there is no export step at all: SWRS is ManageAmerica’s official collections partner, with a live API built into their platform — tag an account for collections inside ManageAmerica and the complete record, signed lease and SODA included, arrives in our intake instantly. Outreach begins within 24–48 hours of a documented placement.
Credit reporting — the rental advantageWe report to Equifax and Experian, including Experian RentBureau, the rental-specific exchange. On former-resident balances this is the single strongest lever in the program: the balance follows the household into its next rental application, which is where a large share of voluntary resolution comes from. Most agencies bidding this work do not carry RentBureau reporting.
Documentation & disputesA disputed move-out balance pauses recovery, it does not end it. The accounts that resolve are the ones that arrive itemized — rent, utilities, fees and damages separated, with the ledger and the deposit disposition attached. Where your file carries that detail, disputes close in days instead of months, and the account survives validation cleanly.

What actually moves the number

Age at placement and itemization, not pressure

The two portfolios at the bottom of that table are not worked differently from the ones at the top. They arrived older and thinner. A former-resident balance is most recoverable in the weeks right after move-out, while the forwarding address is current and the household is applying to rent somewhere else.

The age curve

Ledgers placed within 30–90 days of move-out recover a multiple of what the same ledgers return after a year of sitting in a write-off queue.

The file standard

Itemized balances with the lease, the move-out inspection and the deposit disposition attached survive validation and close disputes. Unitemized lump sums invite them.

Recovery varies by portfolio. SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample of your communities before any accounts are worked — including telling you which paper is not worth placing.

05 — Built for resident receivables

Built for the move-out ledger

A former-resident balance behaves differently from a trade invoice. There is no ongoing relationship to lean on, the household has moved, and the only durable leverage is accurate location data and rental credit reporting. That is the program we run.

Skip tracing that finds movers

Experian-backed location and phone data built for households that have changed address — the single biggest determinant of whether a move-out balance is recoverable at all.

Experian RentBureau reporting

Rental-specific tradeline reporting alongside Equifax and Experian, so the balance surfaces at the next rental application. Reporting is to Equifax and Experian.

Every resident balance type

Final lot rent and rent, utilities and pass-throughs, late and lease-break fees, and post-move-out damages — one partner, one portal, across all 73 communities.

Compliance your residents feel

FDCPA and Regulation F guardrails enforced inside the workflow, with validation notices, consent and call-frequency checks documented on every account. Your communities carry the reputational risk; we treat it that way.
