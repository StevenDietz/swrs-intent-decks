What it costs. Pure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. The rate scales with the age, balance and volume of what you place, and we quote a real number once we’ve seen a representative sample of your aging.

How we work a silent account. Every account is scored first to set the contact strategy, then worked with compliant, multi-channel outreach — email, live and AI-assisted calls, voicemail and mailed notices — plus skip tracing to re-locate a debtor who has moved or changed numbers. A demand on third-party letterhead alone resolves a meaningful share of accounts your office can no longer reach. Everything runs through a secure 24/7 client portal so you always see status on every placed balance.

Residential and commercial, one partner. Homeowner balances are worked under full FDCPA / Reg F consumer rules; balances owed by GCs, property managers and businesses are worked as B2B trade AR. Where lawful we can report to Equifax and Experian.
