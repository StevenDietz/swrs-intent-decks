01 — Introduction

A recovery partner for the invoices that go quiet after the work is done

To Jo Ann Hauger and the team at Bryan Hauger Consulting — in a team-based consulting model, the money leaves before it arrives. Your associates are paid for the failure analysis, the extrusion troubleshooting, the ASTM work, the deposition prep. The client pays on their own schedule. Every invoice that ages past terms is capital you have already spent.

Professional-services receivables age differently. A manufacturer disputes a shipment; a consulting client goes quiet. The project ends, the sponsoring engineer moves on, accounts payable has no context for the invoice, and a balance that was collectible at 60 days becomes a write-off at 18 months — usually without anyone ever refusing to pay.

Southwest Recovery Services is an outsourced commercial receivables recovery partner, working business-to-business balances on pure contingency since 2004, with recovery reach across all 50 states. You place accounts through a secure portal that takes your invoicing or AR-aging export, and we work them. No recovery, no fee.

03 — Your questions, answered

The five things to settle before you place a single account

Written for a B2B technical consultancy with project-based invoices, expert-witness engagements and clients spread across North America.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Fee structurePure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum, no setup cost to place. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. The rate scales with the age, balance and volume of what you place, and we quote it once we have seen a representative sample of your aging. Nothing is worked until you have agreed to the rate in writing.
2. Disputed invoices & scope argumentsThe most common stall in consulting is not “we won’t pay” — it is “that wasn’t what we agreed to.” A dispute pauses recovery; it does not end it. We validate the balance against the paper: the engagement letter or SOW, the itemized invoice, time and deliverable records, and the email trail. For technical work, your reports, test data and correspondence are usually decisive. Dispute investigation typically adds 30–60 days.
3. Clients who simply stop respondingThe core of what we do. Persistent multi-channel outreach, commercial skip tracing to locate the operating entity, its successors and the principals behind a business that has gone dark or reorganized, and review for legal escalation on accounts that still will not engage. A documented third-party demand on agency letterhead resolves a meaningful share of silent commercial accounts on its own.
4. Placement & onboardingLow-lift. Bulk-upload an invoice or AR-aging export from QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks or a spreadsheet through the portal — we map your columns, so nothing is re-keyed. Outreach begins within 24–48 hours of a documented placement. Multiple divisions and d/b/a entities can be placed under one client relationship and reported together or separately.
5. Geography & your brandRecovery reach is nationwide across all 50 U.S. states, which matches a client base spread across North America; international balances route through our legal-forwarding network case by case. Every contact is made in a manner consistent with a firm whose stated product is a satisfied client — compliant outreach, documented, with no threats and no misrepresentation.

The age curve

Why the placement date matters more than the collector

A commercial balance is most collectible while the engagement is still fresh in the client’s mind and the people who authorized it are still in their jobs. Every quarter it ages, the sponsoring contact leaves, the entity reorganizes, contact data goes stale, and leverage erodes. The highest-return change most firms make is not chasing harder — it is placing sooner.

Fresh accounts

Balances under roughly 90 days past due often resolve inside 30–60 days; an invoice that has sat a year collects a fraction of that — the low end of the range on the left.

A standing rule beats a judgment call

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than deciding account by account, and on pure contingency there is no cost to placing an account that later pays you directly.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio. SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any account is worked.

05 — Built for professional-services & B2B recovery

Cutting the days between delivered and banked

Days Sales Outstanding measures one thing: how long completed work sits as a receivable before it becomes cash. Median B2B DSO runs about 43.5 days and the strongest quartile collects in roughly 25. For a consulting firm that pays its associates on delivery, every month past that is working capital financed out of the firm’s own pocket.

Silent and unreachable clients

Commercial skip tracing on the business, its successors and its principals, plus persistent multi-channel demand for clients who stopped responding when the project closed.

Scope and value disputes

Documentation-driven validation — engagement letters, SOWs, deliverables, test data, the email trail — that separates a genuine dispute from a stall.

Every division under one relationship

Pipe, packaging, injection molding, composites, metallurgy, expert-witness and the d/b/a entities — placed together, reported separately if you want it that way.

Nationwide reach

Recovery across all 50 states from 12 offices in 7 states — matched to a client base spread across North America.
