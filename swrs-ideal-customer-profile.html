01 ICP Snapshot — Who We Win For
Strategic Insight The data splits cleanly into two worlds: high-volume consumer paper (low average balance, low unit yield, volume-dependent) and true B2B/commercial accounts (high average balance, high unit yield, relationship-intensive). SWRS's elite yield performance — 50%+ recovery rate — sits almost entirely in the B2B segment with average balances between $10k–$100k and 1–10 placements. That is the ICP.

Watch Signal: Placement Volume vs. Balance Size High-volume clients (1,000+ placements/yr) with low average balances dilute yield and strain operations. The elite B2B segment (101 clients with 50%+ yield rate AND $10k+ unit yield) averages fewer than 10 placements per year but generates outsized revenue per account. Prioritize depth over volume.
Repeat Placement Signal The highest-LTV clients place 2–5× per year consistently. Top performers like United Allergy Services (20 placements, $471k collected, $23.5k/unit yield), KLLM Driving Academy ($700/unit yield across 2,464 placements), and ClubCorp (15,106 accounts) represent the repeat-client flywheel. Ask: "Does this prospect have a recurring AR problem or a one-time write-off?"

04 Qualification & Disqualification Criteria
✓ Qualified — Move Forward
  • B2B company with invoiced receivables owed by other businesses (not consumers)
  • Average outstanding balance $5,000+ per account (sweet spot: $10k–$100k)
  • Debt is 30–365 days past due — still within recovery window
  • Has a signed contract, purchase order, or service agreement documenting the debt
  • Has tried internal collections (phone/email) and debtor has not paid
  • Owner/controller/CFO is the decision maker — can sign a placement agreement
  • Operating in SWRS-licensed states (all 50 for B2B commercial; 47 for consumer)
  • Not currently in active litigation or bankruptcy proceedings on these accounts
  • Willing to provide documentation: invoices, contracts, payment history, contact info
  • Has repeat/recurring AR exposure — not a one-time emergency write-off situation
  • Industry with clean SWRS compliance coverage (HIPAA for healthcare, FDCPA for consumer)
✗ Disqualified — Do Not Pursue
  • Consumer debt in California, Washington, or Oregon (SWRS does not collect there)
  • Average balance under $500/account with no scale volume (<500 accounts/year)
  • Debt is older than 3 years with no prior collection action (statute of limitations risk)
  • No documentation — verbal agreements only, no invoices or contracts
  • Debtor is already in active Chapter 7 or Chapter 13 bankruptcy
  • Prospect wants SWRS to "buy" the debt (debt purchasing — not our model, contingency only)
  • Primarily individual consumer debts under $1,000 with no volume
  • Industry SWRS is not licensed for in target state
  • Client expects "skip tracing only" or won't authorize full recovery workflow
  • Prospect has already placed accounts with 3+ prior agencies with zero recovery (aged-out portfolio)
  • No legal entity — sole proprietor with no business documentation trail
05 Decision Maker Personas
Primary Buyer
The Business Owner / CEO
Profile

Founder or owner of a $2M–$20M B2B services or trades company. Personally involved in AR. Knows specific customers who owe money. Takes the write-off personally. Has probably tried calling the debtor themselves.

Pain Points
  • Cash flow impacted by 2–5 large unpaid invoices
  • Doesn't want to "ruin the relationship" by being aggressive
  • Tried internal collection; debtor stopped returning calls
  • Doesn't know if it's even worth hiring a collection firm
Trigger Phrase

"I've got $80,000 sitting out there and I can't get them to respond."

Economic Buyer
The CFO / Controller
Profile

Finance leader at a $10M–$50M company. Manages DSO, approves the agency relationship. Wants a process — not a one-time miracle. Comfortable with contingency model. Will compare SWRS to 1–2 other firms.

Pain Points
  • DSO creeping up; aging bucket over 90 days is growing
  • Prior agency relationship underperformed — no visibility, no results
  • Needs compliance comfort (FDCPA, HIPAA, state licensing)
  • Wants reporting/portal access without manual effort
Trigger Phrase

"Our 90-day bucket has grown 30% this year. We need a reliable third-party partner."

Influencer / Champion
The AR Manager / Office Manager
Profile

Day-to-day AR contact at a 10–100 employee firm. Will use the SWRS client portal. Evaluates ease of placement, communication, and follow-through. Has been burned by agencies that disappear after intake. Will recommend SWRS upward if onboarding is smooth.

Pain Points
  • Prior agencies required excessive documentation upfront
  • No status updates after placement — "black hole" experience
  • Manual processes — fax, email, spreadsheets
  • Owner is asking for updates she can't provide
Trigger Phrase

"The last agency we used never gave us updates. We had no idea what was happening."

08 Anti-ICP — Clients We Should Not Take
Revenue Warning The data reveals $107M+ placed by clients who generated near-zero collections. These accounts consume operational capacity, damage yield metrics, and provide no revenue. The pattern is clear: small-balance consumer micro-accounts placed by businesses with no recurring AR process. Avoid onboarding these — even when they ask.
Client ProfileWhy to Avoid
Child care / tutoring / small consumer service (<$200/account, 10–50 accounts) Yield collapses. Kinderoo Learning Academy: 40 placements, $0 collected. KFM Martial Arts: $10 unit yield.
Solo medical practice placing consumer health debt <$500/account HIPAA overhead + small balance = uneconomical. Multiple dental/MD practices in the $0-$35 yield bucket.
Consumer-only portfolio >2 years aged, no prior action 88.7% of recovery happens in the first 30 days. 2-year-aged consumer = single-digit probability.
Real estate investors seeking to collect from tenants in CA/WA/OR SWRS does not collect consumer debt in these states. Hard stop.
Business looking to sell/factor their receivables (not contingency model) SWRS is contingency-only. No debt purchasing, no factoring.
The Yield Math A client placing 200 accounts at $150 average balance generates, at 8% yield: $2,400 collected on $30,000 placed. At 30% fee: $720 revenue. At 12 months of collector time. vs. a Tier 1 B2B client placing one $65,000 invoice at 100% yield: $65,000 collected, $19,500 revenue. One account, one phone call. This is why balance size is everything.
The Right Redirect When a small-balance consumer prospect contacts SWRS, don't just turn them away — qualify for minimum portfolio size. If they have 500+ accounts annually even at low balance, that may reach Tier 3 threshold. If not, refer out and focus on the relationship for when they have a bigger commercial problem.
