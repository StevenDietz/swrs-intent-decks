03 — Your questions, answered

Your four questions — answered directly

Exactly what you asked, in order.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. One itemized balance per family?Yes — one account per family is the right way to place them. Each account is the parent or guardian responsible, with the balance broken out (tuition, team fees, uniforms, bows) and the dates each charge came due. If you have the enrollment agreement or fee schedule the family accepted, send it with the account. It is the paper that settles most questions quickly.
2. What percentage?Pure contingency: a pre-agreed percentage of what we actually recover, and nothing if we recover nothing. The rate depends on the age, size and number of the balances, so we quote it once we have seen your actual 3–5 accounts. Steven gives you the number on the call.
3. Minimums or other fees?$0 upfront, no retainer and no monthly minimum. You pay a percentage only of money we recover. The written quote and a sample service agreement spell out every term before you place anything, so there is nothing to discover later.
4. Texas bond filingWe will confirm our Texas bond filing in writing and send you the documentation with the service agreement, so you have it in hand before any account is placed.
Who we contactOnly the parent or guardian responsible for the account — never the athlete. Outreach is honest, compliant and respectful of the fact that these families are still part of your gym’s community.

Timing

Why earlier placement changes the result

The results above share a pattern. Balances placed while the family is still reachable recover far better than balances placed after they have moved, changed numbers or switched to another gym.

The age curve

A balance that has been unpaid for a few months still has a working phone number and a family that remembers the gym. A year after the season ends it often has neither.

The standing rule

Decide a cutoff, such as 60–90 days after a family stops paying and stops responding, and place everything past it. On contingency there is no cost to placing.

Recovery varies by account age, balance and documentation. SWRS gives an honest read on your actual accounts before anything is worked.
