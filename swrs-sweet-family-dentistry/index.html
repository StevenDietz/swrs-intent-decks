03 — Your questions, answered

The questions a practice owner actually asks

You told us you are looking at third-party collections with balances running 61–90 days out. Here is exactly how that works.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Fee structurePure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. The rate is set once we see a representative sample of your aging; a 0–90 day book earns a better rate than a book of two-year-old balances.
2. How patients are treatedThis is the first thing most practice owners want answered, and rightly so. Every contact is compliant, documented and calm — no threats, no misrepresentation, no pressure tactics. Your patients are your livelihood; a recovery partner who damages that relationship has cost you more than the balance was worth.
3. Insurance vs. patient responsibilityWe separate the two before working anything. A balance unpaid because a claim was underpaid, denied or never worked is an insurance follow-up problem, not a patient one — and we can work the payer side as well.
4. Disputed balancesA dispute pauses recovery, it does not end it. We validate against the paper — treatment plan, financial policy, EOB, ledger — and speak with both sides. Good documentation usually resolves it in 30–60 days.

Why placement timing decides the outcome

The same balance is worth several times more at 90 days than at 24 months

You told us your accounts run 61–90 days out — the most useful fact on your webform, because recovery tracks age more closely than any other variable. A balance placed while the patient still recognises the treatment resolves at a materially higher rate.

The age curve

Fresh balances (under ~90 days) recover at multiples of what a two-year-old account returns — the last two case studies above are the same service, placed years apart.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than letting balances drift toward write-off — and on contingency there is no cost to placing.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked.

05 — Built for dental practices

We speak your ledger

A dental book has a specific recovery profile — small balances, high volume, and patients you want back in the chair. Our playbook is built for exactly that.

Patient balances after insurance

Co-pays, deductibles and treatment-plan balances left after the claim settles — worked with the discretion a family practice requires.

Insurance follow-up, not just collections

Underpaid, denied and never-worked claims chased on the payer side, so you are not pursuing a patient for money an insurer owes.

High-volume, small-balance books

Hundreds of modest balances is the profile automation handles best — consistent, documented contact at a cost that still makes sense on a $400 account.

Patients you want to keep

Compliant, respectful contact designed to settle the balance without ending the relationship — most patients pay once someone finally asks clearly.
