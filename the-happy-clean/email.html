Our pricing structure

We work on pure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, and no monthly minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. One note in fairness: a balance outstanding since April is now in the aged band, which typically recovers below a fresh account — still very much worth pursuing on contingency, and a good reason to place future slow-pays sooner.

How sending us items to collect works

It’s low-lift. You see every step live in the portal.
