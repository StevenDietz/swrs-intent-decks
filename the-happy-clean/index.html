The rate scales with the age, balance and volume of what you place; we quote your exact rate once we’ve seen a representative sample of your aging. Our processA documented, escalating sequence: we validate the account against your paperwork (service agreement, work orders, itemized invoices, the communication trail), then work it through persistent, compliant multi-channel outreach — letters, calls, email and SMS — on our AI-driven platform. The age advantage

Why placing sooner recovers more

A commercial balance is most collectible while it’s fresh; every month it ages, contact data goes stale and leverage erodes. Your account has been outstanding since April — already in the aged band — which is exactly why placing it now, rather than waiting further, matters.

The age curve

Fresh accounts (under ~90 days past due) often resolve in 30–60 days; balances that have sat several months collect a fraction of that — still worth pursuing, but with realistic expectations.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than letting balances drift toward write-off — and on contingency there is no cost to placing early.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked. We do not publish blended portfolio averages.
