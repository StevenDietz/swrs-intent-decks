01 — Introduction

Recovering the invoices your partner schools stopped paying

To the RedefinED team — thank you for reaching out. You told us two partner schools are carrying balances past 90 days on services already delivered. Southwest Recovery Services is a commercial, B2B-focused outsourced receivables recovery partner working business-to-business balances on pure contingency since 2004, with recovery reach across all 50 states. You place accounts through a secure portal that takes your invoice or A/R export, and we work them. No recovery, no fee — and your advancement team never has to be the one chasing.

03 — Your questions, answered

Your questions — answered directly

What matters most when the debtor is also a partner school you intend to keep serving.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Fee structurePure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. Commercial contingency scales with the age, balance and volume of what you place; we quote your rate once we see a representative sample. On two accounts totaling roughly $39,000, there is no cost to placing and no cost if we recover nothing.
2. Protecting the school relationshipThis is the first question every services firm asks, so we answer it before pricing. Recovery is handled as a professional business-to-business matter between two organizations — documented, courteous, and escalated in stages. No threats, no misrepresentation, no pressure on individual staff. In practice a third-party letter on agency letterhead changes who at the school is looking at the invoice, and that alone resolves a meaningful share of quiet accounts. If a school comes current and wants to continue with you, nothing in our process prevents that.
3. Balances already past 90 daysBoth of your accounts sit in the 61–90 and 90+ bands, so we will be straight about what that means: a 90+ commercial balance recovers at the lower end of our range, not the top. It is still very much workable — institutional debtors have stable addresses, boards, and audited finances, which is why they behave better than most aged consumer paper — but the honest read is that placing at 90 days recovers materially more than placing at 180. We give you an age-based read on your file before anything is worked.
4. Non-responsive schoolsThe core of what we do: persistent multi-channel outreach, commercial skip tracing to identify the operating entity, the board or diocese behind it, and the finance officer with actual signing authority, and review of legal escalation for accounts that still will not engage. Institutional debtors that ignore a vendor frequently respond once the request reaches the business office rather than the program contact.
5. Onboarding & placementFast and low-lift. Bulk-upload an invoice or A/R export through our portal; we map your columns, so there is no re-keying. Send the service agreement and the invoices alongside it — on B2B accounts, the paper is usually decisive. Outreach begins within 24–48 hours of a documented placement, and recovery reach is nationwide across all 50 states, which matters for a partner base spread across PA, FL, VA, TN, TX, CO and beyond.

The age curve

Why the 90-day mark decides most of the outcome

A commercial balance is most collectible while it is fresh; every month it ages, the contact who authorized the work moves on, budgets close, and leverage erodes. The highest-leverage change a services firm makes is not chasing harder — it is placing sooner.

Where your file sits

Accounts under ~90 days past due often resolve in 30–60 days. Your two balances are already in the 61–90 and 90+ bands, which is workable but sits toward the lower half of the range above.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy across all 200+ partner schools recovers materially more than letting balances drift toward write-off — and on contingency there is no cost to placing.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked.

05 — Built for service-firm receivables

Cutting the days between delivered and paid

Days Sales Outstanding measures how long delivered work sits as a receivable before it becomes cash. Median DSO runs about 43.5 days and the strongest quartile collects in roughly 25. For a lean advancement firm carrying $50K–$250K a month in A/R, every month past that is working capital funding somebody else’s school year.

Schools that have gone quiet

Commercial skip tracing on the institution, its governing board or diocese and its finance officers, plus persistent multi-channel outreach for accounts that stopped responding.

Disputed or contested invoices

Documentation-driven validation — service agreements, scopes of work, deliverable records — that separates a genuine dispute from a budget stall.

Every commercial balance type

Annual program fees, enrollment and admissions retainers, renewal invoices and unbilled overages — one partner, one portal.

Nationwide reach

Recovery across all 50 states from 12 offices in 7 states — built for a partner-school base spread across the country.
