03 — Your questions, answered

Your six questions — answered in order

Exactly what you asked, in the order you asked it. Anything below that needs to be in a signed writing will appear verbatim in the placement agreement.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Will you accept this New York commercial account?Yes. We accept commercial and B2B accounts nationwide, including New York, and we work commercial balances of any size — there is no minimum balance and no volume requirement. Commercial B2B obligations are not consumer debt, so the FDCPA consumer framework does not govern this file; we nonetheless work every account to the same compliant, documented, honest standard.
2. What contingency percentage?Pure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. The commercial rate scales with the age, balance and volume of what you place, so we quote your rate once we have seen a representative sample of your aging rather than publishing a number that will not fit your file. Your rate is fixed in the placement agreement before a single account is worked.
3. Can you pursue the $500 base plus the contractual +2.5%?We can pursue whatever your agreement actually entitles you to collect, and we routinely demand contractual interest, service charges and fee provisions alongside principal. Whether the +2.5% is recoverable in full turns on the exact wording of the provision and on New York law — we are not your counsel and will not confirm enforceability sight-unseen. Send us the clause with the contract and we will tell you plainly, before placement, what we will demand and what we would expect to actually collect.
4. Do you owe anything if we recover nothing?Nothing. Zero. There is no upfront fee, no placement fee, no administrative or membership fee, no closure fee and no cost for accounts we are unable to resolve. Our fee is a percentage of funds actually recovered and remitted. If the account closes with no recovery, no invoice is ever issued. This is stated in writing in the placement agreement.
5. Will any legal or expense-generating step be taken without our authorization?No. Suit referral, legal forwarding and any step that creates a cost or an out-of-pocket exposure for you requires your prior written authorization, account by account. Nothing is escalated on our own initiative. Standard recovery — compliant written demand, telephone outreach, commercial skip tracing on the debtor entity and its principals — carries no charge to you and proceeds under the contingency agreement alone.
6. What do you need to review and place the account?The signed contract or agreement including the provision creating the +2.5%; the invoice or statement of account; the debtor's legal entity name, address and any contact detail you hold; a payment history showing what was paid and what remains; and the correspondence trail including your final demand. That is enough to open the file. Nothing needs to be re-keyed — documents load through our secure client portal.

Why timing decides the outcome

A contested commercial balance is most collectible while it is fresh

You have already done the two things that matter most: you documented the obligation and you issued a final demand. The variable now is age. Every month a commercial balance sits, contact data goes stale, the paying entity reorganizes, and leverage erodes.

The age curve

Fresh commercial accounts — under roughly 90 days past due — often resolve in 30 to 60 days. Balances that have sat a year recover a fraction of that.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than letting balances drift toward write-off — and on contingency there is no cost to placing.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio. SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on what is realistically collectible before any account is worked.
