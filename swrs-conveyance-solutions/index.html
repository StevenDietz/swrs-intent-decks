The honest read on one account

What separates the wins from the write-offs

You should hear this from us rather than discover it later: on a single commercial account the outcome is close to binary — it resolves or it doesn’t, and the average across a one-account placement is not a meaningful number. Two things move the odds, and neither is how hard anyone dials.

Documentation

The accounts above that recovered in full were the ones that arrived with a signed agreement, clean invoices and proof the work was delivered. Thin paper is the single most common reason a good commercial balance stalls.

Age at placement

Fresh commercial balances often resolve in 30–60 days. A balance that has sat a year or more has stale contacts, a debtor who has re-budgeted around it, and materially lower odds — regardless of who works it.

We will give you a straight, documentation-based read on your account before it is worked — including telling you if we think it is a poor candidate. On contingency, we have no reason to take a file we do not believe in.
