03 — Your questions, answered

The questions a contractor actually asks — answered directly

What happens to the invoices you hand us, in plain terms, for an HVAC and plumbing book that runs both residential and commercial work.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Fee structurePure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. The rate scales with the age, balance and volume of what you place; we quote it once we’ve seen a representative sample of your aging.
2. Customers who’ve gone silentThis is the core of what we do. Persistent, compliant multi-channel outreach, plus skip tracing to re-locate a customer — or the business behind a commercial account — that has moved, changed numbers or stopped answering. A demand on third-party letterhead alone resolves a meaningful share of accounts your office can no longer reach.
3. “I’m not paying — the work was wrong”A dispute pauses recovery, it doesn’t end it. We validate the balance against your paper — the signed estimate or work order, the itemized invoice, service notes and photos — and talk to both sides. For trade work that documentation is usually decisive; a genuine dispute is separated from a stall tactic and worked accordingly.
4. Residential and commercial, togetherBoth lanes, one partner. Residential homeowner balances are worked under full FDCPA / Reg F consumer rules; commercial balances owed by GCs, property managers and businesses are worked as B2B trade AR. You don’t split the book across two vendors.
5. Onboarding & placementFast and low-lift. Bulk-upload an aging export from HousecallPro, QuickBooks or your billing system through our portal — we map your columns, so your office isn’t re-keying invoices. Outreach begins within 24–48 hours of a documented placement.

The age curve

Why placing at 90 days beats waiting

A trade balance is most collectible while it’s fresh — the customer still remembers the job, the contact data is still good, and the leverage is still there. Every month it ages, all three erode. Your book is mostly 31–90 days, which is exactly the window where recovery is strongest.

Fresh vs. aged

Accounts under ~90 days past due often resolve in 30–60 days; balances left to sit a year collect a fraction of that.

A standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than letting balances drift toward write-off — and on contingency there’s no cost to placing.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked.
