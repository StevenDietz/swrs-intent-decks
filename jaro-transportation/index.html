01 — Introduction

A recovery partner for the freight invoices that go quiet

To Brian Vennetti and the Jaro finance team — for a carrier, the hardest dollars to collect are the ones owed after the load is delivered: shipper and broker invoices that age past terms, short-pays and deductions that never get resolved, and accounts that simply stop answering once the freight has moved.

Past-due freight receivables aren’t a dispatch problem — they’re a cash-flow problem. Every invoice that drifts past terms ties up working capital you’ve already earned, on equipment and drivers you’ve already paid for.

Southwest Recovery Services is an outsourced receivables recovery partner focused on commercial, B2B accounts. We work business receivables on pure contingency since 2004, with recovery reach across all 50 states and an office in Columbus, Ohio. You place accounts through a secure portal that takes your billing / AR-aging export, and we work them. No recovery, no fee.

Placement timing

Why fresh freight invoices recover best

A commercial balance is most collectible while it’s fresh; every month it ages, contact data goes stale and leverage erodes. The highest-leverage change most finance teams make isn’t chasing harder — it’s placing sooner.

The age curve

Fresh accounts (under ~90 days past due) often resolve in 30–60 days; balances that sit a year collect a fraction of that.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than letting balances drift toward write-off — and on contingency there’s no cost to placing.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked.
