Pricing, first

We work on pure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum and no per-account fee. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. The rate scales with the age, balance and volume of what you place, and I can put a real number against your file once I see a representative sample of your aging.

How it actually works

Every account is scored first to set the contact strategy, then worked with compliant outreach across email, live and AI-assisted calling, voicemail drops and mailed notices — plus a secure 24/7 client portal where you can see status any time.

The workflows are real-time rather than a fixed schedule. No reply to an email triggers the next channel automatically. A missed call triggers a personalized voicemail and a follow-up email. Still nothing, and the account routes to skip tracing or legal review. The result is more right-party contacts, faster settlements and higher recovery than a call-list approach.

Why print receivables behave the way they do

In commercial printing the cost is sunk before the invoice ages — paper, plates, press time and freight are already spent. A broker or agency that collected from the end client and went quiet is holding your margin. We skip trace the operating entity and its principals, and we work corporate AP directly on slow-pay accounts: matching invoice to PO and receipt, chasing the approval that stalled, and escalating to the buyer of record.

On a quality or quantity dispute, the paper trail decides it — the signed estimate or PO, the approved proof, the press sign-off, and the BOL or delivery confirmation. A dispute pauses recovery; it doesn't end it.

What we'd need from you

  • An AR aging export from PrintVis or your accounting system — we map your columns, so nothing gets re-keyed.
  • The estimate or PO and any signed approval for each account placed.
  • Invoices and statements, plus the BOL or delivery confirmation.
  • Whatever contact detail you have for the buyer and their AP department.

All of it uploads through the portal in bulk. Outreach begins within 24–48 hours of a documented placement.

Two other things worth knowing

Beyond third-party recovery we also handle first-party AR management — working your current receivables in your name before they ever age into a placement — and insurance and third-party payer follow-up where that applies. Several clients start with a placement file and add AR management once they see the aging curve flatten. Where lawful, we also report to Equifax and Experian.
