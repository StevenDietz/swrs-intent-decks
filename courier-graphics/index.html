01 — Introduction

A recovery partner for the invoices that go quiet after the job ships

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Fee structurePure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum, no per-account fee. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. Commercial rates scale with the age, balance and volume of what you place; we quote your rate once we see a representative sample of your aging.
2. Disputes over the printed pieceA quality or quantity dispute pauses recovery — it doesn’t end it. We validate the balance against the paper trail: the signed estimate or PO, the approved proof, the press-approval sign-off, the BOL and delivery confirmation, and the communication record. We work AP directly: matching invoice to PO and receipt, chasing the approval that stalled, and escalating to the buyer of record with a documented, professional demand rather than another CSR voicemail.

The age curve

Why fresh print invoices recover best

A commercial balance is most collectible while it is fresh; every month it ages, contact data goes stale, the approving buyer moves on and leverage erodes. The highest-leverage change most print shops make is not chasing harder — it is placing sooner.

Fresh vs. aged

Accounts under roughly 90 days past due often resolve in 30–60 days; balances that sit a year collect a fraction of that — which is exactly what the 9% card above looks like.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than letting balances drift toward write-off — and on contingency there is no cost to placing.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked.

05 — Built for commercial print receivables

We speak DSO — and we reduce it

Every past-due print invoice is a DSO problem before it is a collections problem. The Hackett Group puts median DSO at 43.5 days, while the top quartile collects in 25.1 — those firms touch their cash more than 18 days sooner. Manufacturing and distribution typically runs 45–60 days. When we recover an aged balance it comes off your receivables the day it clears, which moves the metric directly. The 60-day escalation rule

Internal follow-up handles 15–30 days past due and management takes 30–60. Past 60, a documented third-party escalation recovers materially more than another CSR voicemail — and on contingency it costs nothing to place.

Brokers & print resellers

Commercial skip tracing on the business and its principals, plus persistent multi-channel demand for resellers who collected from the end client and went quiet.

Disputed & short-paid jobs

Documentation-driven validation — signed estimate, approved proof, press sign-off, BOL — that separates a genuine quality claim from a stall tactic holding your DSO up.

Every commercial balance type

Catalog and magazine runs, instruction manuals, direct-mail campaigns, storefront and fulfillment billing, freight and kitting charges — one partner, one portal, one number to watch.
