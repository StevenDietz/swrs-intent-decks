A recovery partner for the loans that charged off — without spending the trust you’ve built

But for a lender whose brand is community trust, how those dollars come back matters as much as whether they do.

The age curve on charged-off paper

Why age is the single biggest driver on a written-off loan book

A charged-off loan is most collectible while the borrower is still findable and the trail is warm. Every additional month past charge-off, contact data goes stale, guarantors move, and leverage erodes — which is exactly why a 90-plus-day book recovers toward the low end of the range and a fresher, well-documented file recovers many times higher.

The fresher accounts

The under-one-year notes in your file — with current borrower data and intact guaranties — are where the real recovery lives; they behave like the upper end of the spread above.

The aged tail

The two-to-three-year charged-off paper recovers low no matter who works it; the honest play is to place it now on contingency while any contact data is still valid, rather than let it keep aging toward zero.

Recovery varies by portfolio; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample of your charged-off file before any accounts are worked.
