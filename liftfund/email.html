As promised, here’s a short overview of how Southwest Recovery Services would recover LiftFund’s charged-off small-business loans — and, just as important for a lender whose brand is community trust, how we do it without spending the goodwill you’ve built with the entrepreneurs you serve.

How your borrowers are treated comes first.

Every account is worked with dignity and discretion — respectful, documented contact, never threats or misrepresentation. We’re built on the idea that people pay faster when they’re treated like people, and the work is designed to protect LiftFund’s standing while the money comes back.

What the work looks like.

Persistent, compliant multi-channel outreach; skip tracing on the business, its successors and the principals and guarantors behind a loan that’s gone quiet; and, for documented accounts that still won’t engage, pre-legal review, legal escalation, support for litigation to judgment, and collateral recovery where a security interest exists — the full path you outlined. Consumer / sole-proprietor balances are handled under FDCPA and Reg F; commercial balances under commercial rules; every account worked only where its type can be lawfully collected.

On fees.

Pure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover, and the rate scales with the age and balance of what you place. We’ll give you a real number once we’ve seen a representative sample of your charged-off file.

One honest thing about an aged book.

Your file runs older, and charged-off, 90-plus-day paper recovers materially lower than fresh receivables — there’s no getting around it. But the under-one-year notes, with current borrower data and intact guaranties, are where the real recovery lives, and on contingency there’s no cost to letting us prove it on a sample. We’ll give you an honest, age-based read before you commit to anything.
