01 — Introduction

Receivables management for the invoices your team can’t keep chasing

Prepared for Fitnessmith — thirty years, 125+ equipment partners, four distribution centers and 23,000+ clients across all 50 states. At that scale the receivable is never just one invoice: it is a project balance, an installation draw and a recurring preventative-maintenance contract, billed across health clubs, hotels, apartment communities, country clubs, schools and municipal rec centers — and every one of them pays on its own calendar. A book that wide produces a long tail of small, recurring service invoices no internal AR desk has the hours to chase.

Money past terms is still money you earned. Every month a receivable ages, contact data goes stale and leverage erodes — and internal AR teams can only chase so far before the cost of collection outweighs the balance. That is true of a stalled equipment balance and of a monthly PM contract that quietly stopped paying — and across 23,000+ client relationships, the quiet ones add up faster than the loud ones.

Southwest Recovery Services works both halves of the problem on pure contingency since 2004, nationwide across all 50 states: accounts receivable management keeps you in front of invoicing so payments arrive continually and predictably, and debt recovery goes after the balances that have already gone past terms. Place accounts through a secure portal that takes your AR export, and we work them. No recovery, no fee.

The DSO advantage

Why fresh balances recover best

A commercial balance is most collectible while it’s fresh; every month it ages, contact data goes stale and leverage erodes.

The age curve

Fresh accounts (under ~90 days) often resolve in 30–60 days; balances that sit a year collect a fraction of that.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more, and on contingency there’s no cost to placing.
