01 — Introduction

For a client weighing their options

Daniela — you asked for our process, rates, upfront costs, documentation requirements and timelines so your client can compare agencies side by side. This overview covers all of it, in a form you can forward without editing.

The short version: pure contingency, no upfront cost of any kind. No retainer, no setup fee, no placement fee, no minimum, no monthly or annual fee. If nothing is recovered, nothing is invoiced — the fee comes only out of dollars actually collected, so Southwest Recovery carries the cost and the risk of working the file.

Southwest Recovery Services has worked business and consumer receivables on pure contingency since 2004, from 12 offices across 7 states with six in Texas. Burleson and Tarrant County are core operating territory. One caveat worth stating plainly: a contingency rate is priced against the file. Until we know whether the balances are consumer or commercial, how many there are, how large, how old and what documentation exists, any percentage quoted is a guess — and a guess is worth nothing to a client comparing real offers.

03 — Your questions, answered

Your questions — answered directly

Process, rates, upfront costs, documentation and timelines, in the order you asked.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. The collection processPlacement, then review. We validate the file before any outreach, then work it through a documented sequence: a written notice carrying the consumer or commercial validation language required by law, followed by coordinated letter, phone, email and SMS contact timed to when a debtor is most likely to engage. Skip tracing through Experian data locates a current address, phone and employment when someone has gone quiet. Where authorized and where the account qualifies, balances can be reported to Equifax and Experian. Everything is logged and auditable on every account.
2. Rates and feesPure contingency. Your client is quoted a single stated percentage that appears on the agreement and is agreed in writing before a single account is placed, and nothing else is ever billed. The percentage itself is priced against the file — consumer versus commercial, balance size, account count, age since last payment and documentation quality all move it. We quote once we have seen a representative sample, and we quote honestly rather than low.
3. Upfront costsNone. No retainer, no setup fee, no placement fee, no minimum, no monthly or annual fee, and no upfront legal expense. If we recover nothing, your client is invoiced nothing — no clawback, no minimum draw, no cancellation charge. Accounts can be recalled at any time in writing without penalty; the only carve-out is that contingency stands on dollars already recovered.
4. Documentation to beginThe contract or signed agreement, the itemized invoice or statement, the payment ledger with the date of last payment, any change orders or approvals, the written communication trail, and any demand already sent. Plus debtor identifiers: full legal name or exact registered entity name, service and mailing address, phone and email. If the agreement carries an interest or late-fee provision, that page matters — it may be recoverable. Thin files still get worked; complete files recover materially better.
5. Timelines and other termsOutreach typically begins within 24 to 48 hours of a documented placement. Fresh accounts, inside roughly 90 days of last payment, often resolve in 30 to 60 days; balances that have aged a year recover a fraction of that, which is the single strongest argument for placing sooner. Settlements require your client’s authorization — per account, or inside a standing floor they set. Legal escalation is optional and never happens without written authorization; we are not a law firm, so suit runs through a Texas-licensed attorney and any court costs are disclosed and authorized in advance.

What actually moves the number

The two variables your client controls

A comparison between agencies usually turns on the quoted percentage. In practice the percentage matters far less than these two.

Documentation

A contract, an itemized invoice, a clean payment ledger and the written trail turn an objection into a resolvable conversation. Thin files get worked; complete files recover materially more.

Age at placement

A balance placed inside 90 days of last payment recovers a multiple of one placed at a year. On contingency there is no cost to placing sooner, which makes waiting the most expensive choice available.

Recovery figures vary by account age, balance, documentation quality and industry. Southwest Recovery gives an honest read on a file before it is worked, and says so when an account is unlikely to recover.

Every account SWRS works runs on Aktos, a next-generation, AI-driven collections platform — with compliance built into the workflow, not bolted on afterward. It’s how a boutique, people-first agency delivers enterprise-grade speed, consistency, and auditability.

AI workflow automation

Repetitive steps are automated and every account is scored and routed by predicted payment likelihood — so the right strategy reaches the right account first, without manual queue-picking.

Omnichannel outreach

Coordinated email, SMS, and voice, timed to when a debtor is most likely to engage — replacing high-friction call blasts with respectful, data-driven contact.

Compliance built in

FDCPA, Reg F, and TCPA guardrails are enforced inside the automation, with phone-number and consent checks before outreach — documented and defensible on every account.

Real-time dashboards

You see status, segmentation, and recovery performance on your portfolio in real time — full transparency, not a monthly PDF.

API-first intake

Secure SFTP/API placement means accounts start working without data lag — roughly 50% faster acknowledgment and validation of new placements.

Data-driven scoring

Dynamic scoring and segmentation predict payment likelihood and concentrate effort where recovery is most achievable — earlier, better-targeted contact that resolves accounts faster and reduces disputes.

The result is the combination clients rarely get from a legacy agency: faster cash and cleaner compliance at the same time. Automation accelerates recovery and standardizes documentation, while empathy-first, data-driven contact protects your brand and keeps disputes and complaints low.
