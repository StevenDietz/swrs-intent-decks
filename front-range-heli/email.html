You asked three things: how our process works, what we charge, and whether this can be done without damaging a five-star reputation. How your former students will be treated. That is scoped before anything else, not after. Every account is worked with respectful, documented contact under FDCPA, Reg F and TCPA guardrails that are enforced inside the workflow rather than left to an individual’s judgment — no threats, no misrepresentation, no pressure tactics, no contact outside legal hours. Before a single account is worked, you can review the outreach we intend to use and tell us to change it. Our founding standard is that recovery preserves goodwill and represents the client professionally, and the conduct record is auditable account by account. If someone ever complains, you have the documentation.

One thing specific to flight training. The person who owes you sat in your aircraft and flew with your instructors, and a fair number of them still want to finish the rating. If a former student wants to come back, tell us and we will structure the resolution so the door stays open. On a book your size that is usually the better commercial outcome, and it is not how most agencies would handle it.

How the process runs. Placement, then validation, then structured outreach. We confirm the balance against your paperwork — the training agreement, block-time purchase, invoices, the email trail — send the required validation notice, then work a coordinated sequence across email, mail, SMS and voice, timed to when a person is actually likely to engage rather than on a fixed call schedule. Where someone has moved for a regional job or gone home after training, skip tracing finds current contact data so distance stops being the reason a balance dies. Most of what resolves does so in the first 30 to 90 days.

On cost: pure contingency. $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover, so there is no risk in placing and no cost if we do not collect. The rate scales with the age, balance and volume of what you place, and we quote your real number once we have seen a representative sample of your aging rather than a brochure figure.

Your $500 to $25,500 spread does not need splitting. Smaller balances respond to volume-efficient digital outreach; a lapsed block-time purchase or an unfinished commercial rating justifies individual handling and negotiated payment arrangements. Same portal, same contingency, different intensity.

Something I would rather say now than on the call. Age at placement drives recovery far more than effort does. What we would need: a spreadsheet export with name, contact details, balance, the dates, and what the training was — plus the signed training agreements, which on flight-training balances are usually what resolves the account. We map your columns, so there is no re-keying. Work begins within 24 to 48 hours of a documented placement, and everything runs through a secure client portal with real-time status.
