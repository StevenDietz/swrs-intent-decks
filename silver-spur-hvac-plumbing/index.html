01 — Introduction

A recovery partner for the invoices a homeowner stops answering

To Becky Hennessey and the Silver Spur finance team — the hardest dollars a home-services contractor collects are the ones owed after the work is already in the ground: the $99 service call that never gets paid, and the $20,000 system replacement where the homeowner goes quiet once the equipment is running.

A past-due homeowner balance is not a customer-service problem — it is working capital you have already spent. Parts, labor, truck time and warranty exposure are all committed on day one. Every month the balance ages, contact data goes stale and the recoverable share falls.

Southwest Recovery Services has worked receivables on pure contingency since 2004, from 12 offices in 7 states, with an active book of residential HVAC, plumbing, roofing and home-services contractors. You place accounts through a secure portal that takes your existing aging export. No recovery, no fee.

03 — Your questions, answered

Your questions — answered in order

The twelve items on your list, grouped and answered directly for a residential HVAC and plumbing book across DFW and Houston.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Home-services experienceThis is an active vertical for us, not an adjacency. Our current book includes residential HVAC, plumbing, roofing, pool, electrical and irrigation contractors — including multi-market Texas operators placing homeowner balances at the same $99–$20,000 spread you described. Case results from that book appear later in this overview, low performers included.
2. Recovery rates & timelinesRecovery is driven more by age at placement than by any other single factor. Fresh accounts — under about 90 days past due — commonly resolve within 30 to 60 days. Balances that sit a year or more recover a fraction of that. We give you an honest, age-based read on a representative sample of your aging before a single account is worked, rather than a headline percentage that does not survive contact with your file.
3. Fees & additional costsPure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum, no charge on anything we do not collect. Credit bureau reporting, the client portal, reporting, remittance and pre-collection letters are all included at no additional cost for a portfolio of your type. The only costs that ever sit outside contingency are court, filing and recording fees on accounts you specifically authorize for legal or lien action — quoted per account, in advance, never incurred without your written approval. A full fee schedule accompanies this overview.
4. Small and large balancesOne process, one rate, no balance floor. A $99 service invoice and a $20,000 system replacement are both worked; small balances are handled with a letter-and-call cadence that keeps the cost of recovery proportionate, while larger balances get documentation review, skip tracing and, where you authorize it, escalation. We do not cherry-pick a portfolio and return the small accounts.
5. Pre-collection, payment plans & settlementOur Early-Out program works accounts in the roughly 15-to-60-day window before they ever reach full collections — address validation and NCOA scrub, a structured notice series, professional outbound contact, and accounts returned or escalated at your direction. Payment plans and settlements are negotiated inside the authority Silver Spur sets at onboarding; anything outside it comes back to you for approval.
6. Credit reporting, liens & legal escalationWe report and update qualifying consumer accounts to Experian and Equifax under FCRA and Metro 2 — included, no setup fee. Lien-eligible accounts are identified at placement and routed to our filing partner or counsel; Texas homestead lien rights are narrow and we will tell you plainly which of your accounts actually carry them and which are better served by credit reporting and statutory interest. Nothing goes to an attorney or to a lien filing without your written approval and a per-account quote.

Why the spread is this wide

Age at placement explains almost all of it

The difference between the 83% result and the 6% result on this page is not effort and it is not the trade — it is how old the balances were when they arrived. Contractors who place on a fixed schedule land at the top of this range; contractors who place only after a year of in-house chasing land at the bottom.

The age curve

Balances under ~90 days past due commonly resolve in 30–60 days. A book placed at 12–24 months behaves like the low end of this page, and we will say so before you place it.

The standing rule

A written “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than letting balances drift toward write-off — and on contingency there is no cost to placing early.

Recovery varies by portfolio. SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample of your aging before any account is worked.
