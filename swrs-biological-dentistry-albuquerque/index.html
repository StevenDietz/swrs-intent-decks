01 — Introduction

A recovery partner for the balances that outlived the treatment plan

To Faith and the team at Biological Dentistry of Albuquerque — thank you for reaching out. A biological practice carries a receivables profile most dental offices do not: SMART protocol amalgam removal, ozone therapy, biocompatibility testing and restorative work are largely elective and largely self-pay, so there is no insurance backstop when a patient stops paying.

Self-pay balances are bigger and they are entirely yours to carry. A multi-visit mercury removal case or a full restorative plan is not a $200 co-pay — and when it ages past 90 days, the practice absorbs the whole amount, not the patient portion of it.

Southwest Recovery Services is an outsourced receivables recovery partner working healthcare and patient balances on pure contingency since 2004. You place through a secure portal, and we work them. No recovery, no fee.

03 — Your questions, answered

What a practice owner should ask before placing

Including the honest answer about a book that is entirely 90+.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Fee structurePure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. Balances you have already reserved against cost nothing to attempt.
2. What a 90+ book realistically returnsYou told us your aging is entirely 90+. That matters more than any other detail on your form. Our dental book runs from roughly 45% down to 15%, and the practices at the top placed at 60–90 days. A book that is wholly past 90 belongs in the lower half of that range. We would rather set that expectation now than quote you an average you will not see.
3. How your patients are treatedThe first question most wellness-focused owners ask, and rightly. Your patients chose you for a philosophy of care, and many found you through referral or community. Every contact is calm, compliant and documented — no pressure tactics, no threats. A collector who embarrasses a patient costs you more than the balance.
4. Disputed balancesA dispute pauses recovery, it does not end it. We validate against the treatment plan, the signed financial agreement and the ledger, and speak with both sides. For elective and biocompatible work, a clear written consent and fee agreement usually settles it inside 30–60 days.

Why the last row is the one to read

The same balance, placed a year apart, is a different account

The 45% and the 15% were worked by the same people to the same process. What changed was when the balance arrived. A patient balance placed at 90 days still has a working phone number and a recent memory of the treatment; at two years it has neither.

Your existing 90+ book is worth placing — at zero cost it can only beat the zero it currently returns — but expect the lower half of the range.

05 — Built for dental practices

Cutting the days between treated and paid

Days Sales Outstanding and Average Collection Period measure the same thing: how long completed treatment sits as a receivable before it becomes cash. A book that is wholly past 90 days sits far outside any healthy band, and every further month erodes both the recoverable value and the working capital behind it.

Recovery run on an AI-driven platform — Aktos

Every account SWRS works runs on Aktos, a next-generation, AI-driven collections platform — with compliance built into the workflow, not bolted on afterward. It’s how a boutique, people-first agency delivers enterprise-grade speed, consistency, and auditability.

AI workflow automation

Repetitive steps are automated and every account is scored and routed by predicted payment likelihood — so the right strategy reaches the right account first, without manual queue-picking.

Omnichannel outreach

Coordinated email, SMS, and voice, timed to when a debtor is most likely to engage — replacing high-friction call blasts with respectful, data-driven contact.

Compliance built in

FDCPA, Reg F, and TCPA guardrails are enforced inside the automation, with phone-number and consent checks before outreach — documented and defensible on every account.

Real-time dashboards

You see status, segmentation, and recovery performance on your portfolio in real time — full transparency, not a monthly PDF.

API-first intake

Secure SFTP/API placement means accounts start working without data lag — roughly 50% faster acknowledgment and validation of new placements.

Data-driven scoring

Dynamic scoring and segmentation predict payment likelihood and concentrate effort where recovery is most achievable — earlier intervention that returns more of each aging balance and reduces disputes.

The result is the combination clients rarely get from a legacy agency: faster cash and cleaner compliance at the same time. Automation accelerates recovery and standardizes documentation, while empathy-first, data-driven contact protects your brand and keeps disputes and complaints low.
