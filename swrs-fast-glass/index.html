03 — Your questions, answered

Your four questions — answered directly

Exactly what you asked, in order, plus the two things that matter most for a mixed residential-and-commercial glass book.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Fee structurePure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly fee, no charge on what we don’t collect. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. The rate scales with the age, balance size and volume of what you place, so we quote it once we’ve seen a representative sample of your aging rather than guessing at it now.
2. Account minimumsNone. No minimum balance per account and no minimum placement volume. A $600 residential glass balance is worth placing; so is a $22,000 commercial glazing invoice. Small balances are worked through automated, compliant outreach rather than being priced out of the file.
3. Collection processPlace the account with the invoice or work order, the signed estimate or contract, and last known contact details. We review it, skip-trace where the contact data has gone stale, then run documented multi-channel outreach — letter, phone, email — into payment-plan negotiation, and review escalation to pre-legal on accounts that justify it. You see status on every account live in the portal and can recall an account at any point.
4. Consumer and commercial togetherBoth, under one agreement, routed automatically by debtor type — you don’t sort them. Homeowner balances run the consumer track: validation notice, Reg F call-frequency limits, cease-communication honored, documented and auditable. Contractor, property-manager and commercial invoices run the B2B trade track, which sits outside the FDCPA and allows direct engagement with AP, the controller and ownership.
The two that decide the outcomeDocumentation and timing. A signed estimate or work order and a clean invoice turn a “they never authorized that” dispute into a paid account. And placing at a fixed trigger instead of when the aging finally gets cleaned out is the single largest lever on what you recover. One note on geography: we don’t work consumer accounts where the debtor resides in California, Oregon or Washington — those are returned at intake.

Why timing beats effort

The aged accounts you have now are the hardest ones you’ll ever place

You mentioned a handful of aged accounts. Those are worth placing — but they are the low end of the range above, not the high end, and we would rather say that now than let you infer 49%. The value of this relationship is in what you place next year, not what has already gone cold.

The age curve

A balance placed inside 90 days past due frequently resolves in 30–60 days. The same balance at 18 months collects a fraction of that, because contact data is stale, the customer has moved on and your leverage is gone.

The standing rule

Pick a trigger — 90 days is the common one — and let everything cross it automatically. On contingency there is no cost to placing early and no penalty when an account pays you directly first.

Recovery varies by portfolio, aging mix and documentation quality. SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked, and we do not guarantee recovery rates.

05 — Built for glass & glazing receivables

The four ways a glass shop’s money gets stuck

Trade receivables in glass fail in predictable ways, and each one needs a different move. Chasing all four the same way is why in-house AR stalls out.

The homeowner who went quiet

Small residential balances worked on the consumer track — skip tracing, compliant multi-channel contact, payment plans sized to what a household will actually complete. Treated with the same courtesy you showed them on the job.

The GC waiting on their own draw

Commercial skip tracing on the operating entity, its successors and its principals, plus direct engagement with AP and ownership. Pay-when-paid is a negotiating position, not a defense.

The disputed job

Documentation-driven validation — signed estimate, work order, change orders, photos, the message trail — that separates a real workmanship dispute from a stall. Send us the paper and most of these close.

Property managers and repeat accounts

Portfolio-level contact that recovers the balance without burning a relationship you intend to keep billing. Firm, professional, never a liability to your name in the Denver market.
