Fee structure, first, because you asked. Pure contingency — the only model we have run since 2004. No upfront cost, no setup fee, no retainer, no monthly minimum, no per-account charge. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover, and nothing at all on accounts we don’t collect. The rate follows the age, average balance and volume of what you place, so I’ll quote you a real number once I’ve seen a representative sample of your delinquent Club Plan aging rather than guess at a range in an email.

How it works — not just phone calls. Every account is scored before anyone contacts it, and that score sets the contact strategy. From there it’s compliant outreach across text, email, live and AI-powered calls, voicemail drops and mailed notices, all of it running through our secure 24/7 client portal. You place accounts by uploading the delinquency export Vantaca already produces — we map your columns to ours, so nothing is re-keyed and no format change is required on your side. Club Plan agreements, ledgers, statements and chargeback documentation go into the same portal, and outreach begins within 24–48 hours of a documented placement.

The workflows run in real time. No reply to an email triggers an SMS automatically. A missed call triggers a personalized voicemail and a follow-up email. An account that still won’t engage moves to skip tracing — address, phone and employment through national databases, plus property-record work that is unusually effective on a dues book, because the obligation follows a home you can already locate — and from there to legal review. None of it runs on a fixed monthly schedule; it’s timed to when that particular account actually engages. The result is more right-party contacts, faster settlements and higher recovery. Payment arrangements are set up and administered inside limits you define — minimum payment, maximum term, settlement authority — they post to the portal in real time, an arrangement that breaks is re-engaged automatically instead of going quiet, and a member who pays MetroLagoons directly is logged the same day and stops being contacted.

Post-chargeback guest balances — yes, with a condition. Once an issuer decides a dispute for the cardholder the card-network remedy is finished, but the underlying obligation between MetroLagoons and that guest normally survives it. We can pursue it where you can produce the transaction record, the terms the guest accepted at purchase, and the issuer’s final decision. Where that documentation is thin, recovery runs materially below what your dues book will do — so I’d scope, price and report chargebacks as their own portfolio rather than blend them into the dues numbers and flatter both.

On compliance: FDCPA and Regulation F guardrails are enforced inside the workflow rather than bolted on afterward, a dispute pauses recovery and gets written validation plus a documented investigation against the Club Plan agreement, and we furnish to Equifax and Experian where lawful and only after a validation notice has run. SMS specifically requires documented prior express consent, so I’ll want to see the consent language in your Club Plan enrollment before a single text goes out — where it isn’t there we work email, phone and mail, and I’ll tell you rather than assume. Your communities carry the reputational risk on every one of these accounts and we work them that way. We’ve collected millions of dollars in industries just like yours since 2004.
