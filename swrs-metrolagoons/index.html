01 — Introduction

A recovery partner for dues owed by people who still live here

To Bryan Nadeau and the MetroLagoons team — a delinquent Club Plan account is not an anonymous balance. It belongs to a homeowner who lives in the community, whose neighbours are at the Lagoon on Saturday, and who you want back in good standing rather than gone. That changes how the account has to be worked, and it is the first thing we want to talk about — before fees, before process.

Recurring dues behave differently from one-off receivables. A Club Plan balance doesn’t sit still — it compounds every billing cycle a member stays delinquent, so a book that goes unworked for two quarters is materially harder and more expensive to resolve than the same book placed at 90 days. The single highest-leverage change most dues operators make is not chasing harder; it is placing on a fixed schedule.

We also note the distinction you drew: these are Club Plan dues tied to residential homeownership, not HOA or CDD assessments. That matters operationally. We work them as consumer contract receivables under FDCPA and Regulation F — validation notice, documented dispute handling, contact-frequency limits — rather than assuming any statutory assessment remedy. The Club Plan membership agreement is the controlling document, and it is the first thing we will ask to see.

Southwest Recovery Services is an outsourced receivables recovery partner working consumer and commercial balances on pure contingency since 2004, from 12 offices across 7 states including Florida. You place accounts through a secure portal that takes your existing export — no re-keying. No recovery, no fee.

Your questions — answered directly

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Fee structurePure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum, no per-account fee. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. The rate is driven by the age, average balance and volume of what you place; we quote it once we have seen a representative sample of your delinquent Club Plan aging. Nothing is charged for accounts we don’t collect.
2. How members are contactedPhone, email, SMS and physical mail, sequenced by what the account responds to rather than by volume. Tone is the point: these are your residents, and every contact is made as a professional representative of MetroLagoons, not against it. SMS specifically requires documented prior express consent — we will want to see the consent language in your Club Plan enrollment before a single text goes out, and we work email, phone and mail where it isn’t there.
3. Payment arrangementsSet up and administered end to end — our collectors are authorised to negotiate terms inside limits you define, and we will hold to whatever floor you set on minimum payment, maximum term and settlement authority. Payments post to your portal in real time, and arrangements that break are re-engaged automatically rather than falling silent.
4. Disputed accountsA dispute pauses recovery; it does not end it. We log it, send written validation, and work it against the paper — the Club Plan agreement, the billing and payment history, and the communication trail. For a dues book the agreement usually settles it quickly. Disputes are documented account-by-account and reported back to you, so you see the pattern as well as the outcome.
5. Skip tracing and escalationStandard on every placement, not an add-on: address, phone and employment tracing through national databases, plus property-record work that is particularly effective here, since the obligation follows a home you can locate. Where normal recovery is exhausted we review the account for credit reporting (Equifax and Experian) and, with your written authorisation account-by-account, legal escalation through our attorney network.
6. Reporting and Vantaca intakeYou place accounts by uploading your existing export — we map your columns, so a Vantaca-generated delinquency file goes in as it comes out. Inside the portal you get live account status, recovery-to-date and remittance detail on demand, plus direct-payment logging so a member who pays MetroLagoons directly stops being contacted the same day. Reporting is self-service and real-time, not a monthly PDF.
7. Post-chargeback guest balancesYes — with a condition worth stating plainly. Once an issuer decides a dispute for the cardholder, the card network remedy is finished, but the underlying obligation between MetroLagoons and that guest usually survives it. We can pursue it where you can produce the transaction record, the terms the guest accepted at purchase, and the issuer’s final decision. Where that documentation is thin, recovery rates are materially lower than on your dues book — so we would scope, price and report chargeback placements as their own portfolio rather than blending them into the dues numbers.

The dues advantage

Why a recurring-dues book rewards placing on a schedule

A Club Plan balance is unlike a one-time invoice: it grows every cycle the member stays delinquent, and the member’s willingness to engage falls as the number gets larger. The gap between the top and bottom of the case studies above is almost entirely age at placement.

The age curve

Accounts placed inside ~90–120 days of delinquency recover several times what the same accounts return after a year of internal follow-up. The balance has not yet compounded past what the member believes they can pay.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy, applied automatically out of Vantaca, recovers materially more than case-by-case decisions — and on pure contingency there is no cost to placing early.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio. SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample of your delinquent Club Plan accounts before any of them are worked.

05 — Built for membership & dues recovery

Recovering the money without losing the member

A delinquent member is still a resident, a neighbour and — in almost every case — a future paying member. The whole discipline of recovering residential dues is doing it firmly enough to work and carefully enough that the person comes back into good standing rather than out of the community angry.

Recurring Club Plan dues

Compounding monthly balances worked on a schedule, with arrangements that fit what a household can actually carry and automatic re-engagement when one breaks.

Non-responsive members

Skip tracing on phone, address and employment, plus property-record work — the obligation follows a home, which makes a dues account materially easier to locate than most consumer debt.

Disputed and contested balances

Documentation-driven validation against the Club Plan agreement and billing history, logged account-by-account and reported back so you see the pattern behind the disputes.

Post-chargeback guest balances

Scoped, priced and reported as their own portfolio — pursued on the underlying obligation and the documentation behind it, never on the card-network remedy that has already closed.
