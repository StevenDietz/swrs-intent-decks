01 — Introduction

The invoices that stall when the project does

To Anita Torres and the team at Urban Strategy — you told us you have roughly $500,000 outstanding. In land development that number rarely means anyone refuses to pay. It means the grading and drainage design is finished, the ALTA survey is delivered, the plans are stamped — and the project sponsor is waiting on financing, on entitlements, or on their own capital partner.

Engineering receivables age for a structural reason. Your work is front-loaded: the design and the survey land early, the developer’s money arrives late. Many projects are held in single-purpose entities that go quiet when the deal stalls, and the project manager who authorized your scope moves to another firm. The invoice does not get disputed. It just stops moving.

Southwest Recovery Services is an outsourced commercial receivables recovery partner working business-to-business balances on pure contingency since 2004, from 12 offices across 7 states with recovery reach in all 50. You place accounts through a secure portal that takes your accounting or AR-aging export. No recovery, no fee.

03 — Your questions, answered

What a $500,000 book actually needs

Written for a civil engineering and surveying firm billing developers, contractors, architects and public agencies across five states.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Fee structurePure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum, no cost to place. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. The rate scales with the age, balance and volume of what you place, and we quote it once we have seen a representative sample of your aging. On a book this size, that sample conversation is worth having before you place anything.
2. Single-purpose entities and stalled projectsThe defining problem in land development AR. A project LLC goes dark and the balance looks uncollectible — but the principals, the parent developer and the successor entity usually are not hard to find. We run commercial skip tracing on the entity, its registered agent, its officers and its affiliates, then work the party that actually has the money rather than the shell that signed the contract.
3. Scope and change-order disputesThe common stall in engineering is not refusal, it is “that was not in the original scope.” A dispute pauses recovery, it does not end it. We validate against the paper — the executed proposal, the change orders, submitted deliverables, the plan-review and revision trail. In your work the deliverable itself is usually decisive: the stamped plans were issued and the survey was recorded.
4. Public agencies and prime contractorsWork billed to municipalities and to primes behaves differently from work billed to a developer. Agency payables move slowly but reliably and usually need process pressure, not legal pressure. Balances behind a prime contractor need the chain documented. We handle these separately rather than running one script across the whole book.
5. Multi-state and legal escalationRecovery reach is nationwide, which matches your licensure across Texas, Oklahoma, Louisiana, Arizona and Colorado. Legal escalation is reviewed case by case and never without your sign-off. On construction and development balances there are often statutory remedies with hard deadlines attached — if you have not had counsel review those on the older invoices in this book, that is worth doing in parallel with placement, not after.

The age curve

On a book this size, the placement date is worth more than the collector

A commercial balance is most collectible while the project is still live and the people who authorized the work are still in their jobs. Every quarter it ages, the project entity reorganizes, the developer’s PM moves firms, contact data goes stale and leverage erodes. The highest-return change most firms make is not chasing harder — it is placing sooner.

Fresh accounts

Balances under roughly 90 days past due often resolve inside 30–60 days; an invoice that has sat two years collects a fraction of that — the low end of the range on the left.

A standing rule beats a judgment call

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than deciding account by account, and on pure contingency there is no cost to placing one that later pays you directly.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio. SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any account is worked.

05 — Built for engineering & professional-services recovery

Cutting the days between stamped and paid

Days Sales Outstanding measures one thing: how long completed work sits as a receivable before it becomes cash. Median B2B DSO runs about 43.5 days and the strongest quartile collects in roughly 25. For a 32-person firm carrying payroll, licensure and survey crews against work already delivered, every month past that is working capital financed out of the firm’s own pocket.

Stalled projects and silent entities

Commercial skip tracing on the project LLC, its principals, its parent and its successors — so you are working the party with the money, not the shell.

Scope and change-order disputes
