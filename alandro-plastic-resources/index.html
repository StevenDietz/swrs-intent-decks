03 — Your questions, answered

Your two questions — answered directly

Cost first, then exactly what a placement file needs. Everything below applies to both of the balances you named.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. What it costsPure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum, no cost to place. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover; if we collect nothing, you owe nothing. The rate scales with the age, balance and volume of what you place, and we quote it once we have seen a representative sample of your aging — not before, because quoting blind serves neither of us.
2. What information we needLess than most people expect. Per account: the debtor’s legal business name and DBA, billing and physical address, a contact name with phone and email, the invoice number(s), invoice date, original balance, any payments applied, and the current balance. That is the minimum to begin work.
What makes it work fasterSupporting documents lift recovery materially on commercial accounts: the signed purchase order or contract, the itemized invoice, proof of delivery or bills of lading, weight or scale tickets, certificates of analysis or spec sheets, and the email trail showing the balance was acknowledged. On a disputed load, the scale ticket and the COA usually settle it.
3. Disputed loadsA dispute pauses recovery — it does not end it. We validate the account against the paper and speak with both sides. Contamination and off-spec claims on material that has already been processed are the most common commercial dispute we see, and documentation decides them. Investigation typically adds 30–60 days.

The age curve

Why a fresh trade invoice recovers best

A commercial balance is most collectible while it is fresh. Every month it ages, the buyer’s contact data goes stale, the people who took delivery move on, and the leverage the paperwork gave you erodes. The highest-value change most accounting offices make is not chasing harder — it is placing sooner.

Fresh versus aged

Accounts under roughly 90 days past due often resolve in 30–60 days; balances that have sat a year recover a fraction of that.

A standing rule beats a judgment call

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than letting balances drift toward write-off — and on contingency there is no cost to placing.

Recovery varies by portfolio. SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked — including telling you when a balance is not worth placing.

05 — Built for industrial & trade AR

Cutting the days between shipped and banked

Days Sales Outstanding measures one thing: how long material you have already shipped sits as a receivable before it becomes cash. Median DSO runs about 43.5 days and the strongest quartile collects in roughly 25 — every month past that erodes both recoverable value and the working capital that funds your next buy.
