1. What it costs

Pure contingency. $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum, and no cost to place an account. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover — if we collect nothing, you owe nothing. The rate scales with the age, balance and volume of what you place, and we quote you a real number once we have seen a representative sample of your aging. Quoting before that would be a guess, and you would have no way to hold us to it.

2. What we would need from you

Less than most people expect. Per account, the minimum to begin work is:

  • Debtor’s legal business name and DBA
  • Billing and physical address
  • A contact name with phone and email
  • Invoice number(s), invoice date, original balance, payments applied, current balance

What moves the needle beyond that: the signed PO or contract, the itemized invoice, proof of delivery or bills of lading, weight and scale tickets, certificates of analysis or spec sheets, and the email trail showing the balance was acknowledged. On a contaminated or off-spec load, the scale ticket and the COA usually decide the outcome.

How the accounts are actually worked

Each account is scored first to set the contact strategy, then worked through compliant outreach across text, email, live and AI-assisted calls, voicemail drops and mailed notices, with a secure 24/7 portal on your side throughout. The workflows respond in real time — no reply to an email triggers a text; a missed call triggers a personalized voicemail and follow-up email; an account still not engaging goes to skip tracing or legal review. Timing follows how that specific account behaves rather than a fixed calendar, which is what produces more right-party contacts and faster settlements.

Everything runs under FDCPA, Reg F and TCPA guardrails built into the workflow, with credit reporting to Equifax and Experian where lawful. We’ve collected millions of dollars in industries just like yours since 2004.
