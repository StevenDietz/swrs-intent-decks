The placement-timing advantage

Why a fresh service invoice recovers best

An HVAC balance is most collectible while the job is fresh in the customer’s mind; every month it ages, contact information goes stale and leverage erodes. The highest-leverage change most contractors make isn’t chasing harder — it’s placing sooner.

The age curve

Accounts under ~90 days past due often resolve in 30–60 days; balances that sit a year collect a fraction of that — true on both the homeowner and the trade side.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than letting balances drift toward write-off — and on contingency there’s no cost to placing early.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked.
