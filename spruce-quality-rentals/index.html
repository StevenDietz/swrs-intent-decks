03 — Your questions, answered

Your four questions — answered directly

In the order you asked them, including where the honest answer is “it depends on the agreement.”

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Individual residential ownersYes, owner balances under a management agreement are in scope for review. Our core is commercial and B2B recovery, so we first classify each account from the agreement and the owner’s purpose (investment property is typically a business relationship; a personal-residence arrangement can be treated differently). Where consumer rules apply, outreach follows them, and we confirm scope by account type and state before any account is placed.
2. Minimums & documentationWe set no fee minimum. What matters is the file: the signed management agreement, the owner ledger or statement of charges, invoices and receipts behind each reimbursable expense, proof the owner was billed, and the communication trail. A representative sample tells us which balances are worth working.
3. Fees & upfront costsPure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. The rate is quoted once we have seen a representative sample of your aging.
4. Disputed balancesYes. A dispute pauses recovery; it does not end it. We validate the charge against the paper — agreement terms, owner approvals for expenses, invoices and the ledger — and speak with both sides. Dispute investigation typically adds 30–60 days, and well-documented charges resolve fastest.

The placement advantage

Why fresh balances recover best

A balance is most collectible while the relationship and the paper are fresh; every month it ages, contact data goes stale and leverage erodes. The highest-leverage change most teams make isn’t chasing harder — it’s placing sooner.

The age curve

Fresh accounts (under ~90 days past due) often resolve in 30–60 days; balances that sit a year collect a fraction of that.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than letting balances drift toward write-off — and on contingency there’s no cost to placing.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked.
