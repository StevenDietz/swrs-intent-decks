2. Minimum balance or documentation? We set no fee minimum. What matters is the file: the signed management agreement, the owner ledger or statement of charges, the invoices and receipts behind each reimbursable expense, proof the owner was billed, and the communication trail. A representative sample tells us which balances are worth working.

3. Contingency fee and upfront costs? It is pure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. 4. Disputed balances? Yes. A dispute pauses recovery rather than ending it. We validate the charge against the paper — agreement terms, owner approvals for expenses, invoices and the ledger — and speak with both sides. Investigation typically adds 30–60 days, and well-documented charges resolve fastest.
