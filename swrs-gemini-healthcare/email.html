How your residents and families are treated. For a skilled nursing operator this is the first question, and it should be. Every account we work runs under FDCPA, Reg F and TCPA guardrails that are enforced inside the workflow — validation notices, contact-frequency limits and consent checks are automated, not left to an individual collector's judgment. Your name is on the account, so how a family experiences the outreach is our problem as much as yours.

Pricing. Pure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. The rate scales with the age, balance size and volume of what you place, and we will quote a real number once we have seen a representative sample across the buildings.

How the work actually happens. Each account is scored first to set the contact strategy, then worked with compliant outreach across text, email, live and AI-powered calls, voicemail drops and mailed notices, with a secure 24/7 client portal behind it. The workflows are real-time rather than on a fixed schedule: no reply to an email auto-triggers an SMS, a missed call triggers a personalized voicemail plus email, and continued silence routes the account to skip tracing through our Experian data stack or to legal review. That timing is what produces right-party contacts on a family that moved after discharge.

An honest word on aging. In long-term care, recovery tracks the age of the file more than the size of the balance. In our seasoned skilled-nursing book, portfolios placed while contact data was still good recovered in the 18–21% range; books placed after a year or more of internal effort ran 6–8%. If your bad debt is largely 12 months and older, expect the lower end — I would rather you hear that now than on the call. We report to Equifax and Experian where it is lawful and appropriate, subject to the medical-debt reporting rules and waiting periods. We've collected millions of dollars in industries just like yours since 2004.
