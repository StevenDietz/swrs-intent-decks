01 — Introduction

A recovery partner for the balances that outlast the stay

Jimmy — thank you for reaching out. In skilled nursing, the hardest dollars to collect are the ones still outstanding after a resident has discharged, passed, or spent down: private-pay coinsurance and room-and-board shares, Medicaid patient-liability that was never remitted, responsible-party balances where the family has stopped responding, and denied or short-paid days your business office has already worked twice.

These balances don’t age like a trade invoice — they age faster. Once a resident leaves the building, the address on the face sheet goes stale, the responsible party stops answering, and the account quietly moves toward write-off while your business office is busy with the current census.

Southwest Recovery Services is an outsourced receivables recovery partner working healthcare balances on pure contingency since 2004. You place accounts through a secure portal that takes an aging export straight out of PointClickCare, MatrixCare or your billing system — we map your columns, so nothing gets re-keyed. The aging curve in long-term care

Why the discharge date matters more than the balance

The single strongest predictor of recovery on a resident balance is not how large it is — it is how long it sat after discharge. The books above that recovered 18–21% were placed while contact data was still good. The books at 6–8% were placed after the account had already been worked internally for a year or more.

Where the spread comes from

Fresh post-discharge balances still have a working phone number and a responsible party who recognizes the facility name. A file that has sat 18 months has neither, and it collects like the bottom of that range.

The standing rule

A fixed policy — place at 90 or 120 days post-discharge rather than letting balances drift — moves a book materially up this range. On pure contingency there is no cost to placing sooner.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio and by aging mix. Before any accounts are worked we give you an honest, age-based read on a representative sample from your facilities — including telling you where we think recovery will be thin.
