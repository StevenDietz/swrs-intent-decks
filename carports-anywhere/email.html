Thank you for the detailed request — it is the most complete list of the right questions I have seen from a manufacturer this year, and I will answer every one of them in order. Southwest Recovery Services is an outsourced receivables recovery partner; we have worked consumer and commercial balances on pure contingency since 2004.

One framing point first, because it shapes every answer below. You are carrying two different receivables: individual customers who took delivery of a structure and stopped paying, and dealers and resellers sitting on past-due trade invoices. The consumer half is regulated debt — FDCPA, Regulation F, state licensing. The dealer half is commercial trade credit and is not. We segment those into separate work streams on day one, because they do not respond to the same treatment.

1–3) Contingency rates and fees. Pure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum, no setup fee, no per-account fee. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. Consumer and commercial are priced on separate schedules, and yes, rates do vary with the age and balance of the account — a 60-day balance and a three-year-old balance are not the same asset. Skip tracing, credit reporting, letters, calls, portal access and reporting are all included in the contingency rate; none of them are billed separately. Legal escalation is the only exception: if an account warrants suit we bring it to you first with a recommendation, and court costs and any attorney arrangement are disclosed and approved by you in writing before anything is filed. We never advance legal costs without your consent. I will quote your actual percentages once we have seen a representative sample of your aging, because the honest number depends on what is in the file.

4) When the fee is earned. On any payment on a placed account received after placement — including a payment a customer makes directly to Carports Anywhere. That is the industry standard and it exists because our demand is usually what produced the payment.

5) Reporting direct payments. Report them to us promptly; within two business days is the working standard. You can log them in the portal, email them, or include them on your remittance report. This matters beyond the fee: prompt reporting stops a customer who has already paid from being contacted again, and it keeps the balance accurate for credit reporting.

6) Recall and cancellation. You may recall any account at any time and there is no recall or cancellation fee. The only amount ever owed on a recalled account is the contingency on money already collected, or on a payment made to you after placement.

7) Recall to file a lien. Yes — free, and this is worth being explicit about. If you decide to pursue a lien, a bond claim, litigation or any other remedy, recall the account and the file comes back to you with our work product and contact data at no charge. Placing an account with us does not waive, toll or interfere with your lien rights. Lien deadlines are unforgiving, so tell us at placement which accounts have a live lien window and we will flag and prioritize them so the clock is never the reason you lose the remedy.

8) Settlement authority. Yours, not ours. The default is that we collect the full balance, and nothing is settled or discounted without your prior approval. If you prefer, you can grant standing authority in writing for a defined band — many clients do this so a small consumer balance can be closed on the phone rather than lost to a delay. Payment plans follow the same rule: you set the floor, and every arrangement is visible in the portal.

9) Credit reporting and skip tracing. Both included in the contingency rate, at no additional cost. Consumer credit reporting is furnished to Equifax and Experian under FCRA and Metro 2 standards. It is a portfolio-level election you make at onboarding — on or off for the consumer book as a whole, not an account-by-account decision — and paid or disputed balances are updated accordingly. Commercial and dealer balances are not consumer-reported. Skip tracing runs on both books: locating an individual customer who has moved, and identifying the operating entity, successors and principals behind a dealer that has gone quiet.

10) Where we are licensed. Recovery reach is nationwide for commercial and dealer balances, which are not consumer debt and are not subject to consumer collection licensing. On the consumer side we are licensed and bonded where required, and we do not place consumer accounts in California, Oregon or Washington; Minnesota is restricted. We operate from 12 offices across 7 states — Texas, Oklahoma, Colorado, Missouri, Ohio, Georgia and Florida — including Florida, where your customer base is concentrated. Send your aging and we will tell you before you place exactly which accounts we can and cannot work.

11) Documentation and minimums. No minimum balance and no minimum age. Per account we need the customer or dealer name, last known address, phone and email, the invoice or contract, and an itemized balance with the date of last activity or last payment. For consumer accounts, add the signed agreement and proof of delivery. For dealers, add the credit application and any personal guaranty — a guaranty is the single most valuable document in a dealer file, because it converts a dead corporate balance into a collectable one. Thin documentation will not block placement, but it caps what is recoverable if the balance is disputed.

12) Portal, reporting and your account representative. You get a named client-services representative, not a queue. Every placement runs through the SWRS Client Portal: bulk placement from an aging export (Bill.com, QuickBooks, NetSuite or a spreadsheet — we map your columns, so there is no re-keying), secure document exchange, on-demand account and recovery reporting, and direct-payment logging. Remittance and statements run on a set monthly cycle.

13) Turnaround to first activity. Outreach begins within 24 to 48 hours of a documented placement, and your first compliant contact attempt goes out inside that window.

14) Standard agreement. Attached, with our terms and conditions, for your review.

Because half your book is individual customers in a business that runs on word of mouth, one more thing worth saying plainly: every consumer contact we make is FDCPA- and Reg F-compliant and professional. The objective is payment without costing Carports Anywhere a referral. Dealer accounts get the same discretion, plus documentation-driven dispute handling for any invoice that gets contested.
