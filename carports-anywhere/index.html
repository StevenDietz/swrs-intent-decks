01 — Introduction

Two books, two recovery motions, one partner

To the Carports Anywhere accounting team — thank you for the detailed request. 03 — Your questions, answered

Your questions — answered in order

Every point from your 9/30 request, answered directly. Rate percentages are quoted on the call once we have seen a representative sample of your aging, because the number depends on what is actually in the file.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1–3. Fee structure and feesPure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum, no setup fee, no per-account fee. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we recover. Consumer and dealer carry separate rates, and rates do vary with age and balance. Skip tracing, credit reporting, letters, calls, portal and reporting are included — nothing is billed separately. Legal escalation is the only exception: we bring it to you first, and court costs and any attorney arrangement are disclosed and approved by you in writing before filing.
4–5. When the fee is earned, and direct paymentsThe fee is earned on any payment on a placed account received after placement, including one paid directly to Carports Anywhere — our demand is usually what caused it. Report direct payments within two business days through the portal, by email, or on your remittance report. That stops a paying customer being contacted again and keeps the balance accurate for credit reporting.
6–7. Recall, cancellation, and the lien questionRecall any account at any time; there is no recall or cancellation fee. If you decide to file a lien, pursue a bond claim or litigate, recall it and the file comes back with our work product and contact data. The only amount ever owed is contingency on money already collected or paid to you after placement. Placing does not waive or toll your lien rights — flag live lien windows at placement and we prioritise them.
8. Settlement authorityYours, not ours. The default is that we collect the full balance; nothing is settled or discounted without your prior approval unless you grant standing authority in writing. Many clients pre-authorise a defined band so small consumer balances can close on the phone. Payment plans follow the same rule and every arrangement is visible in the portal.
9. Credit reporting and skip tracingBoth included in the rate. Consumer reporting is furnished to Equifax and Experian under FCRA and Metro 2; it is a portfolio-level election at onboarding — on or off for the consumer book, not per account. Commercial balances are not consumer-reported. Skip tracing runs on both books, including the entity, successors and principals behind a dealer that has gone quiet.
10–11. Licensing, documentation, minimumsNationwide reach for commercial and dealer balances, which are not consumer debt. On the consumer side we are licensed and bonded where required and do not place accounts in California, Oregon or Washington; Minnesota is restricted. 12 offices across 7 states including Florida. No minimum balance, no minimum age. Per account: name, last known address, phone, email, the invoice or contract, and an itemised balance with date of last activity. Consumer adds the signed agreement and proof of delivery; dealers add the credit application and any personal guaranty — the single most valuable document in a dealer file.
12–13. Portal, reporting, dedicated rep, turnaroundYou get a named client-services representative, not a queue. Every placement runs through the SWRS Client Portal: bulk placement from an aging export, secure document exchange, on-demand reporting, and direct-payment logging. Outreach begins within 24 to 48 hours of a documented placement. Remittance and statements run on a set monthly cycle.

Why the low numbers are low

Age at placement, not effort, decides the outcome

The two weakest results above are not different work — they are older files. No amount of pressure closes that gap. The highest-leverage change a manufacturer makes is not chasing harder; it is placing sooner.

The age curve

Balances under roughly 90 days past due often resolve inside 30 to 60 days. A balance that has sat a year collects a fraction of that, because contact data goes stale and the customer has already decided not to pay.

The standing rule

A fixed policy — place at 90 days, every time, no exceptions — recovers materially more than letting balances drift toward write-off. On pure contingency there is no cost to placing early and no penalty for being wrong.

Recovery varies by portfolio. SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample of your aging before any account is worked, and will tell you plainly if part of the file is not worth placing.

Dealer & reseller trade invoices

Commercial recovery with no FDCPA constraint: firm, fast demand, entity and principal skip tracing, and personal-guaranty enforcement when the dealer entity has gone dark.
