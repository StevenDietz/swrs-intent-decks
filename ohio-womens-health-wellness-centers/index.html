Why timing matters

Why fresh balances recover best

A patient balance is most recoverable while the visit is recent; every month it ages, contact data goes stale and the relationship cools.

The age curve

Fresh balances (under ~90 days) often resolve in 30–60 days; balances that sit a year collect a fraction of that.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than letting balances drift — and on contingency there’s no cost to placing.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked.
