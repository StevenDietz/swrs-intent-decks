Hi Yvonne,

Thank you for reaching out about recovery for Thousand Hills Realty. I put together a short overview built around what you told us — credit reporting, skip tracing, legal escalation and payment plans, on a book aging at 31 to 60 days.

That aging is the headline. A former-resident balance is rarely hard to collect because the person refuses. It is hard because they moved and nobody knows where. At 31 to 60 days your move-out paperwork is recent and the contact information on file is usually still good. That is the strongest starting position there is in residential recovery, and it is the opposite of what most portfolios hand us.

On credit reporting — this is the one that matters most in rental. We report to Equifax and Experian, and specifically to Experian RentBureau, the rental-history database that property managers and screening companies check when a former resident of yours applies somewhere else. A balance owed to Thousand Hills shows up on their next application. Nothing is reported until the account has been validated and the person has had their full dispute window.

On skip tracing: address, phone and employment tracing against whatever forwarding information you have, reworked as data refreshes. At 31 to 60 days this is cheapest and most likely to land.

On legal escalation: case by case, never automatic, never without your sign-off. Worth it on a large damage claim with a signed lease, move-out inspection, itemized charges and photographs behind it. Rarely worth it on a small balance — and we will say so rather than run up activity that will not return it.

On payment plans: most former residents who pay do so over time. We set arrangements people can actually keep, and they can self-serve through our secure portal at any hour. That resolves more balances than any demand letter.

On cost: pure contingency. $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover, so there is no risk in placing and no cost if we do not collect. We quote your rate once we have seen a representative sample of your aging.

One thing I want to say plainly. In a market the size of Branson, how a former resident gets treated gets talked about. Every contact we make runs inside FDCPA and Reg F guardrails and is documented — so if someone calls your office upset, your staff can pull up exactly what was said and when. Recovery that costs Thousand Hills its reputation is not recovery.

What we would need: a delinquency or former-resident export straight out of AppFolio. We map your columns, so there is no re-keying and no new workflow for your office. Work begins within 24 to 48 hours of a documented placement.

We have collected millions of dollars in industries just like yours since 2004. If it is useful, we also handle outsourced AR management — so balances can be worked well before they ever reach third-party placement.
