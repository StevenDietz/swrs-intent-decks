The four services you asked about

Taken in the order you selected them on the form, with what each one actually does for a residential portfolio.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Credit reportingThis is the one that changes behaviour in rental. We report to Equifax and Experian, and — specific to residential — to Experian RentBureau, the rental-history database that property managers and screening companies check when a former resident of yours applies somewhere else. A balance owed to Thousand Hills surfaces on their next application. Nothing is reported until the account has been validated and the resident has had their full dispute window.
2. Skip tracingThe whole problem with a former-resident balance is that the person is gone. We run address, phone and employment tracing against the forwarding information you have, and keep working it as data refreshes. At 31–60 days your contact data is still warm, which is exactly when tracing is cheapest and most likely to land.

It is rarely worth it on a small balance, and we will tell you so rather than run up activity on an account that will not return it.
4. Payment plansMost former residents who pay do so over time, not at once. We set structured arrangements the person can actually keep, and they self-serve through our portal at any hour — which resolves more balances than any demand letter does. You see every arrangement and every payment as it posts.

Why 31–60 days matters

The forwarding address is the whole game

A former-resident balance is not hard to collect because the person refuses. It is hard because they moved and nobody knows where. Every month that passes, the forwarding address goes stale, the phone changes, the employer changes. Place while the trail is warm and you are collecting; place at eighteen months and you are investigating.

Where you are now

At 31–60 days the move-out paperwork is recent and the contact data on file is usually still good — the strongest starting position in residential recovery.

A standing rule beats a case-by-case call

A fixed policy of placing at 60 days recovers materially more than deciding account by account, and on pure contingency there is no cost to placing one that later pays you directly.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio. SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any account is worked.
