Why aging matters most in patient AR

The honest read on a 90+ day book

A patient balance is most collectible in the first 90 days after the final statement. Once a share of the book crosses that line — as some of yours has — recovery on those specific accounts settles toward the lower end of the range above; the fresher portion of your book will recover meaningfully better.

The age curve

Balances under 90 days often resolve in 30–60 days; balances over a year recover a fraction of that — which is why placing sooner, not chasing harder, is the highest-leverage change.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy across all three locations recovers materially more than letting balances drift — and on contingency there’s no cost to placing early.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked.
