03 — Your questions, answered

The questions owners ask — answered

How the fee works, what happens when a customer goes silent or moves, how we stay compliant, and how to hand it over.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. How does the fee work?Pure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we recover. Rates are tiered by the age and size of the balances; we quote once we see a representative sample. There’s no minimum to place.
2. The customer went silent — now what?The core of what we do: persistent, professional multi-channel outreach across mail, phone, email and text, plus skip tracing to locate residential customers who have moved or changed numbers — so the demand actually reaches them.
3. How do you stay compliant?Every account is worked inside an FDCPA / Reg F-disciplined process — these are consumer balances, so compliance isn’t optional. Firm, professional contact that never uses threats or misrepresentation, and never puts your name at risk.
4. Does credit reporting help?Yes — where appropriate we report to Equifax and Experian. A reported balance follows a consumer onto their credit file, which is one of the strongest lawful motivators to resolve an old account.
5. How do we hand it over?Fast and low-lift. Place the account through our secure portal with the invoice/agreement and the contact details you have; we map your fields, no re-keying. Outreach begins within 24–48 hours of a documented placement, and you track every account 24/7.

The cost of waiting

Why fresh customer balances recover best

A consumer balance is most collectible while it’s fresh, before contact data goes stale. Research from the Commercial Collection Agencies of America puts collectability at under 25% once a balance passes a year old. The highest-leverage change isn’t chasing harder — it’s placing sooner.

The age curve

Fresh balances (under ~90–120 days) resolve fastest — the customer is findable and the balance is fresh in their mind.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than chasing case-by-case — and on contingency there’s no cost to placing.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on your accounts before anything is worked.
