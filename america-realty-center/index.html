03 — Your questions, answered

Your eight questions — answered in order

Exactly what you asked, in the order you asked it, with the detail that matters for a single-family residential rental book carrying post-eviction judgments.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Single-family residential rentalsYes — a core part of our consumer book. We work former-tenant balances for single-family rental managers, small residential portfolios, multifamily operators and HOAs: unpaid rent, late fees, lease-break and early-termination charges, damage beyond the deposit, billed-back utilities and post-eviction deficiency balances.
2. Contingency ratePure contingency — a pre-agreed percentage of what we actually recover, and nothing on an account that does not collect. The percentage moves with the age, balance and annual volume you place, so we quote once we have seen a representative sample of your aging. Judgment accounts and previously-worked accounts price differently from fresh move-outs, which is why we ask for the sample first.
3. Upfront, setup or placement feesNone. No upfront cost, no retainer, no monthly minimum, no per-account placement fee, and no charge for accounts we fail to collect. The only separate one-time cost is optional enrollment in consumer credit reporting, disclosed in writing with the agreement before you commit.
4. Skip tracing and interstateBoth, and this file needs them most. Skip tracing runs on the Experian stack — address, phone, employment and asset location on a resident who moved without notice. Recovery reach is nationwide across all 50 states, so a tenant who left Georgia stays workable. No separate skip-tracing charge; it is inside the contingency.
5. Credit bureau reportingYes, for eligible accounts. We report to Equifax and Experian, and for rental debt also to Experian RentBureau — the rental-payment database screening companies pull when a former resident applies for their next lease. On rental balances that is frequently the strongest single point of leverage. Reporting is governed by FCRA: accounts validated, disputes investigated, balances updated.
6. Judgments and legal enforcementYes — post-judgment recovery is a distinct track. Be clear on the structure: SWRS is a recovery agency, not your law firm. We pursue the judgment debtor directly through tracing, demand and negotiation; where enforcement needs court action — garnishment, levy, domesticating a Georgia judgment out of state — the account goes to licensed counsel in our legal-forwarding network, on your written authorization, with cost and timeline disclosed first.
7. Settlement authorityYou keep it. Settlements are governed by the authority you grant in the agreement. Many clients pre-authorize a standing discount range so routine settlements close on the phone; anything outside it returns to you in writing. If you want every settlement signed off individually, we set it up that way.
8. Documents to place an accountStandard balance: signed lease, final ledger or move-out statement, itemized deposit disposition, last known address, and any phone or email on file — plus date of birth and SSN where your application captured them, which materially improve both tracing and credit reporting. Judgment account: add the certified judgment, case number and county, and any post-judgment payment record.

What the spread is telling you

Documented and early beats bulk and aged — by an order of magnitude

Read the six results above in order and one pattern runs straight through them. The books that recover at the top of the range are small, individually documented and placed while the trail is still warm. The 3% result at the bottom is a portfolio of more than twelve hundred aged tenant balances dumped in bulk with thin paperwork — the same agency, the same process, a completely different outcome.

Why the trail matters

A former resident is findable for roughly the first 90 days after move-out. After a year, the phone is disconnected, the forwarding order has lapsed and the employer on the application has changed — recovery falls to a fraction of what it was.

Where your file sits

A single documented judgment account with a certified order, a lease and a ledger behind it is the profile that performs at the top of this range, not the bottom. Placing your future move-outs as they happen, rather than at year-end, is the change that compounds.

Recovery varies by portfolio, age and documentation. SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked — including telling you when a balance is not worth placing.

05 — Built for residential rental recovery

Finding the resident is most of the job

Commercial collection is a negotiation with a business that still exists at a known address. Former-tenant collection is a search problem first and a negotiation second — which is why the data stack and the compliance discipline behind it matter more here than anywhere else in receivables.

Skips and gone-no-forwarding

Experian-backed address, phone, employment and asset tracing on a resident who left without notice — the single highest-leverage step on a move-out balance, included in the contingency.

Post-eviction judgment balances

Direct post-judgment recovery, with garnishment, levy and out-of-state domestication routed to licensed counsel in our legal-forwarding network on your written authorization.

Experian RentBureau reporting

Rental-specific credit reporting alongside Equifax and Experian — the balance surfaces when the former resident applies for their next lease, which is when people pay.

FDCPA and Reg F discipline

Contact frequency caps, validation notices, dispute handling and consent checks enforced inside the workflow — documented and defensible, so recovery never becomes your liability.
