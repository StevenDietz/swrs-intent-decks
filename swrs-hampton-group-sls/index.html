03 — Your questions, answered

Your seven questions — answered in order

Exactly what you asked, in the order you asked it, with the detail that matters for a residency-agreement book rather than a healthcare one.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
1. Fee / contingency ratePure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no minimum, no per-account fee. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. The rate follows the age, balance size and volume of what you place, and we quote it once we have seen a representative sample of your aging.
2. Senior living & deceased-resident experienceIndependent and assisted living balances are a standing part of our residential book — monthly service fees, rent through the notice period, community-fee shortfalls, damage and restoration charges. Deceased-resident accounts run on a separate, slower track: death confirmed before further contact, outreach suspended through the early weeks of loss, and the account moved toward the estate.
3. Estates, probate & responsible partiesWe locate the personal representative and present the claim into the estate, not to relatives who owe nothing. Arizona’s non-claim rules put a hard clock on creditor claims, so these are triaged first. Where a third party signed your residency agreement we read the document: a true personal guaranty is pursued on its terms; a signature given in a representative capacity is not, and we will say so.
4. Eviction, judgments & legal escalationYes. Post-eviction balances are standard work, and an existing judgment is an asset — we go straight to post-judgment enforcement through our attorney network. Balances with no judgment are assessed for suit on documentation and asset picture; nothing is filed without your written approval.
5. Onboarding & what we need from youLow-lift. A signed agreement, then a spreadsheet export from your property-management system — resident name, last known address, phone, email, dates of residency and move-out, itemized balance, and the community as creditor of record. Agreements, final statements, ledgers and any death notice or eviction order load into the same portal. Outreach begins within 24–48 hours of a documented placement.
6. Typical recovery timelinesMost of what resolves does so inside 90–180 days of placement, with first payments commonly in the first 30–60. Estate accounts run longer and follow the court’s calendar, not ours. Age at placement is the biggest single driver — see page 05.
7. Credit bureau reportingYes, on eligible accounts — we furnish to Equifax and Experian, and rental and residency balances also to Experian RentBureau, the tenant-screening database other housing operators pull before approving an applicant. Reporting is elected once at onboarding for the portfolio, not account by account, and nothing is furnished until the validation window has run.

The move-out clock

Why the date you place matters more than the rate you negotiate

A former-resident balance is most collectible in the weeks right after move-out, while the forwarding address is current and the ledger undisputed. Every month after that, contact data decays and the account drifts toward the bottom of the range above.

The age curve

Accounts placed within 90–120 days of move-out recover at a materially higher rate than the same balances placed a year later; estate accounts are harder still once the probate claim window has closed.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days past move-out” policy recovers more than letting balances sit on the ledger until year-end write-off — and on pure contingency there is no cost to placing early.

Recovery varies by portfolio and by aging. Before any accounts are worked, SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample of your file — including which accounts we do not think are worth placing.

05 — Built for housing operators

Recovering the ledger without putting your communities in the middle of it

A senior living community sells trust to adult children before it sells anything to a resident. How a past-due balance is handled after move-out is part of that reputation — visible in reviews and family word of mouth long after the account closes. Our job is to collect the money and leave your name intact.

Deceased residents & estates

Death confirmed before further contact, outreach suspended through the early weeks of loss, and the claim presented into the estate against Arizona’s probate clock — not to a grieving family who is not liable.

Responsible parties, read correctly

We read your residency agreement to distinguish a genuine personal guaranty from a signature given in a representative capacity, and pursue only what the document actually supports.

Post-eviction & judgment balances

Existing judgments go straight to post-judgment enforcement; unsatisfied balances are assessed for suit on documentation and asset picture, with nothing filed without your written approval.

Equifax, Experian & RentBureau

Eligible balances furnished to Equifax and Experian, and rental and residency balances to Experian RentBureau — where the next operator screening that applicant will see them.
