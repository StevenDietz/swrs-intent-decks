Thanks for reaching out through our site. The hardest dollars a property-management office collects are the ones a former resident stops paying once they’ve moved on — skipped final balances, damage charges, lease-break fees. That silent balance is what we work every day.

What it costs. Pure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. The rate scales with the age, balance and volume of what you place, and we quote a real number once we’ve seen a representative sample of your aging.

How we work a silent account. Every account is scored first to set the contact strategy, then worked with compliant, multi-channel outreach — email, live and AI-assisted calls, voicemail and mailed notices — plus skip tracing to re-locate a debtor who has moved or changed numbers. A demand on third-party letterhead alone resolves a meaningful share of accounts your office can no longer reach. Everything runs through a secure 24/7 client portal so you always see status on every placed balance.

Resident and commercial, one partner. Resident balances are worked under full FDCPA / Reg F consumer rules; owner and vendor balances are worked as B2B AR — one partner, one portal. Where lawful we can report to Equifax and Experian.

What we’d need to start. An aging export from your property-management system or QuickBooks through the client portal — we map your columns, so your team isn’t re-keying anything — plus the lease, the tenant ledger, and move-out photos or inspection notes for anything you expect to be disputed. Outreach begins within 24–48 hours of a documented placement.
