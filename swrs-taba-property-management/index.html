The age curve

Why placing sooner recovers more

Rent and tenant balances are most collectible while fresh — the resident is still reachable and the ledger is still clean. Every month a balance ages, contact data and leverage erode.

Fresh vs. aged

Accounts under ~90 days past due often resolve in 30–60 days; balances left to sit a year collect a fraction of that.

A standing rule

A fixed ‘place at 90 days’ policy recovers materially more than letting balances drift toward write-off — and on contingency there’s no cost to placing.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked.

AI workflow automation

Repetitive steps are automated and every account is scored and routed by predicted payment likelihood — so the right strategy reaches the right account first, without manual queue-picking.

Omnichannel outreach

Compliance built in
