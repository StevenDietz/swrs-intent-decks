A recovery partner for the invoices your team can’t keep chasing

Prepared for Fit Supply — 18+ years, 50+ equipment manufacturer partners, four distribution centers, 30+ installation partners and projects in all 50 states. 03 — Your questions, answered

What a controller asks us — answered directly

The questions that matter when the book is equipment, installation and recurring service contracts sold into procurement offices that each pay on their own cycle.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
What does it cost?Pure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum. You pay a pre-agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. Commercial contingency scales with the age, balance and volume of your placements; we quote your rate once we see a representative sample of your aged AR.
Which balances should we place?Anything past terms that has stopped moving — post-installation project balances, repair invoices, and preventative-maintenance contracts that quietly lapsed. The 90-plus bucket is where internal effort stops paying for itself and third-party recovery starts.
What about a disputed installation or equipment charge?A dispute pauses recovery — it doesn’t end it. We validate the account against your paper: the quote, the purchase order, the delivery ticket and the signed installation sign-off. That documentation trail is unusually strong evidence in commercial recovery, and it separates a genuine dispute from a stall. Dispute investigation typically adds 30–60 days.
The community changed property managers — who owes us now?Commercial skip tracing locates the operating entity, successors and principals behind a club that sold, a community that changed management, or a facility that went quiet after a rebrand — so the balance stays attached to the obligor instead of aging into a write-off.
How do we hand accounts over?Bulk-upload an AR aging export from your accounting system through the portal; we map your columns, so nothing gets re-keyed. Outreach begins within 24–48 hours of a documented placement, and you can run reports and log direct payments yourself at any time.
Is this consumer debt collection?No. Your receivables are commercial, business-to-business balances, so the FDCPA’s consumer restrictions do not govern them. We still work every account under a documented, professional process — ACA International member, clean compliance record since 2004 — because the delinquent service customer is often next year’s equipment buyer.

The DSO advantage

Why fresh commercial invoices recover best

A commercial balance is most collectible while it’s fresh; every month it ages, contact data goes stale and leverage erodes. The highest-leverage change most finance teams make isn’t chasing harder — it’s placing sooner.

The age curve

Fresh accounts (under ~90 days past due) often resolve in 30–60 days; balances that sit a year collect a fraction of that.

The standing rule

A fixed “place at 90 days” policy recovers materially more than letting balances drift toward write-off — and on contingency there’s no cost to placing.

Recovery figures vary by portfolio; SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked.
