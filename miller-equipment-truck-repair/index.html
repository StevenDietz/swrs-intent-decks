01 — Introduction

For the repair orders that leave the shop and never get paid

To the owner of Miller Equipment & Truck Repair — you run 24/7 emergency and mobile service out of Jeromesville for carriers who are, by definition, on the move. Southwest Recovery Services is an outsourced receivables recovery partner working commercial trade AR on pure contingency since 2004, with recovery reach across all 50 states. You place accounts through a secure portal that takes an export or a simple spreadsheet of open invoices, and we work them — including the out-of-state carriers you have no practical way to chase from a shop in Ashland County. 03 — Your questions, answered

The questions a shop owner actually asks

Straight answers, in the order they usually come up in the bay.

Your questionSouthwest Recovery Services
What does it cost?Pure contingency — $0 upfront, no retainer, no monthly minimum, no per-account fee. You pay an agreed percentage only of what we actually recover. If we collect nothing on an account, you owe nothing on it. The rate is set against the age, size and volume of what you place; we quote it once we have seen a representative sample of your open invoices.
How small is too small to place?There isn’t a floor that matters for a shop your size. A $1,400 road-service call and a $22,000 engine job get the same intake and the same documented process — contingency means the economics work on both. Most independent shops place in small batches as invoices cross 60 or 90 days rather than waiting to accumulate a big file.
The carrier is out of state and won’t answer.This is the core of the work. We run commercial skip tracing on the operating entity, its DOT and MC registrations, successor entities and the principals behind it — then apply persistent, compliant multi-channel outreach. A demand from a third-party recovery firm resolves a meaningful share of accounts that have simply gone quiet on the shop.
They say the repair was wrong or the bill was too high.A dispute pauses recovery, it doesn’t end it. We validate the account against your paper — the signed repair order or work authorization, the parts invoices, tech notes, photos and the text or call trail with the driver or dispatcher. In heavy-truck work the signed authorization and the parts documentation are usually decisive. Dispute investigation typically adds 30–60 days.
What do I have to send you?Less than most owners expect. An export or spreadsheet of open invoices with the customer name, address, phone, invoice number, date and balance is enough to start. Repair orders, authorizations and parts detail get uploaded per account as needed — you do not need to assemble a file before you place. Outreach begins within 24–48 hours of a documented placement.
Will this cost me the customer?Some of these carriers you want back and some you don’t — you tell us which. Recovery is firm and professional, never threatening or misleading, and you can flag any account as relationship-sensitive so we handle it accordingly. Your 4.9-star reputation in Jeromesville is an asset we are not going to spend for you.

Why the spread looks like that

Age is the single biggest variable in a repair-order book

Look at the range on the cards above and the pattern is not random. The shops that recovered 54–63% placed small batches of relatively fresh invoices. The 16% result came from a large file of balances that had already been chased in-house for a year. Nothing about the shops differed much — the timing did.

The age curve

A repair order under ~90 days past due often resolves in 30–60 days. The same invoice at 12 months collects a fraction of that, because the carrier has moved, re-registered, or in some cases stopped operating entirely.

The standing rule

A fixed “place it at 90 days” habit recovers materially more than letting invoices drift toward write-off. On contingency there is no cost to placing early — the only thing waiting protects is the write-off.

SWRS gives an honest, age-based read on a representative sample before any accounts are worked. If your open file is mostly 12 months and older, we will tell you it behaves like the low end of that range rather than let you read 60% off the top card.
