How this report was prepared

Statute. An account is shown as in statute when fewer than six years have passed since its date of record (Colorado period for rent and liquidated debt, C.R.S. §13-80-103.5). Dates are measured exactly as of October 28, 2026.

Credit reporting. Reporting status comes from the credit-bureau transmission records on each account. “Actively reporting” means transmitted within the last 60 days.

Activity. Counts reflect logged letters, calls, emails and skip traces. Internal status entries that carry no contact detail are excluded. 114 logged actions across the six in-statute accounts.
