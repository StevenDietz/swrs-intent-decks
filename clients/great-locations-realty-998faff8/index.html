Industry benchmark data puts the chance of collecting at about 69% at three months past due, 51% at six, 21% at twelve and 9% at twenty-four. Today 7 of the 8 accounts are more than 24 months past their record date.
  • Not responding. Calls and emails have gone unanswered, and several numbers on file are disconnected or belong to someone else (for example, a number on the Wood account that the co-tenant said is not the debtor’s).
  • Contact stopped. One account carries a cease-and-desist request (Mahrous) and one debtor has opted out of email (Robinson). Credit-bureau reporting was removed on the two closed accounts in October 2026.
