The client asked why nothing has been collected since April. The answer is not a lack of effort in volume (336 calls, 355 emails, 81 letters). It is that the people and automation working the accounts did not have what was in the packets.

What we entered and what the documents also held

Reached the account

  • Name and balance
  • An address, often a skip-trace address, not the unit
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses (two were keyed wrong)

Left in the documents

  • Employer, job title and income
  • Second phones and emergency contacts
  • Prior addresses and landlords, and a forwarding address
  • Co-tenants named on the lease
  • Identifiers (SSN, date of birth, license) in several packets
  • Move-out dates and eviction papers
  • Text messages admitting the debt and asking for terms

Identifier fields in ADS cannot be read by our tools; that check is still open.

Walk the right column slowly. Each item is something a collector can use to locate, verify or approach the debtor. The point is not that the data was hidden. It was in the vault the whole time.

The standard: every packet, every page

1

Read every page. OCR the image pages. Nothing is missing until every file is read.

5

Load employer, job title and income, and every prior and forwarding address.

2

Load the identifiers: SSN or tax ID, date of birth, license.

6

Set up every co-tenant and guarantor. For a business: the entity, the owner and the guarantor.

3

Load every phone number that belongs to the debtor, and flag the mobile.

7

Put hardship, promises and texts in an account note with the date.

4

Copy emails from the source one character at a time, then read them back (the letter l is not the digit 1).

8

Check the vault: the debtor’s own packet is there and nothing from another client is.

Based on the SWRS placement data standard: Tier 1 required to work an account, Tier 2 lifts contact rates, Tier 3 supports enforcement

This is the same read-every-page rule already in the placement procedure. The CMRE file shows what happens when only the contact basics are entered.

What we do not do

Load a third party as a contact

Emergency contacts, employers, landlords and relatives go in a note as location leads. They are never a dial or email target.

Contact before checking the flags

Read the notes for cease-and-desist, bankruptcy, attorney and dispute before any contact or approval of an email.

Place rent that is not yet due

Residential accounts: place the balance due to date and flag later months separately. Check late fees against the lease first.

Leave a vault wrong

Another client’s document or a missing packet gets fixed the same day, and a business lease is entered as a business.
