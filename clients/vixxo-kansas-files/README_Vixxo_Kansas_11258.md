## 3. WHY THE OPEN ACCOUNTS STALLED

**85% of the open balance is owed by entities with clear capacity to pay.** Only Harper-Allen was ever claimed to be defunct, and that claim is now contradicted. This book does not have an insolvency problem. Three patterns explain it:

**Stale contacts absorbed the effort.** Pet Retail Brands was chased through a CFO who left over a year ago and a president who retired in 2016. James Construction received 48 emails at an address whose owner departed in February 2026 and left an auto-reply naming her replacement — only 2 went to the live address.

**No escalation above the accounts-payable clerk.** Employbridge, the largest industrial staffing company in the United States, received 37 emails to one branch contact over a $2,694 balance and not one went to Atlanta corporate. Lightning Electric routed all 22 calls back to one person who was avoiding them.

**Unanswered questions to the client blocked the file.** Planet Mars stalled twice on an unresolved personal-guaranty question. Melrose stalled on a short-pay reconciliation nobody produced. Ottobock was closed with a live invoice dispute still open after recovering 90.8%.
