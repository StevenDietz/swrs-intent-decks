Documentation & Compliance Advisory

Why This Portfolio’s Recovery Rate Is Below Benchmark

This portfolio is liquidating at 12.9% against an industry benchmark of 15–20%. The primary driver is a documentation gap. Of the 8 accounts placed, supporting documentation has been uploaded for one — the McKee/Snook lease at 501 McGoodwin, covering 26 files. The remaining 7 accounts, carrying $28,696.62 in open balance, have no lease, ledger or move-out statement on file.

SWRS makes the full initial campaign on every account regardless of documentation, and this portfolio shows that: 1,143 logged actions across 8 files. But when the initial campaign does not produce voluntary payment, federal law limits what we can do next without the underlying records. We cannot verify a disputed balance, we cannot itemize under Regulation F, and we cannot support a litigation referral.

FDCPA § 1692g — a written dispute suspends collection until the debt is verified. Without the lease and ledger, a dispute permanently closes the file.

Reg F § 1006.34 — the validation notice requires an itemization traceable to the underlying account records.

FDCPA § 1692e / § 1692f — late fees, damages and other charges may only be collected where the agreement authorizes them. Without the signed lease those amounts are not defensible.

FCRA § 1681s-2 — furnishing a balance we cannot substantiate creates accuracy exposure for both SWRS and the property owner.

Without documentation

  • Initial contact campaign only
  • Cannot survive a written dispute
  • Credit bureau reporting not supportable
  • Litigation referral unavailable
  • 5-year limitations period (Missouri open account, RSMo § 516.120)
  • Expected recovery: 4–12%

With documentation

  • Full multi-channel campaign with escalation
  • Disputes answered and collection resumed
  • Credit bureau reporting active
  • Litigation referral available on balances above $3,000
  • 10-year limitations period (Missouri written contract, RSMo § 516.110)
  • Expected recovery: 15–20%

Requested: Supporting Documentation

Uploading the following for the eight undocumented accounts would materially change what we can pursue on $28,696.62 of open balance:

  • Signed lease with all addenda and any guaranty
  • Itemized ledger or rent roll showing charges and payments by date
  • Final account statement / statement of deposit account (SODA)
  • Move-in and move-out inspection reports, dated photos, repair invoices
  • Notice to vacate or lease termination notice
  • Any dispossessory filing and resulting judgment (money judgment vs. writ of possession)
